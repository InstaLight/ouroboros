Title: Chapter 15
---
Andrew fell on his back, laughing at the sight. He lifted what used to be his left arm, and found only melting bone in it's place. Tears welled up in his eyes. A normal person would've been killed instantly just from the sheer pain, but all Andrew could do was laugh.\
Mark turned around, facing his foe. The entire upper half of his body, starting at the crystal, was completely enveloped in the roaring flames. His blood that stained the nearby dirt ignited as well, a wall of fire trapping Andrew. Mark took a step forward, the grass in front of him disintegrating.\
Andrew tried to back up, but found that there was nowhere to go. Still laughing, he reached out his still intact right arm, the dark crystal in his wrist pulsating. A barrage of metal object came raining down on Mark, consisting mostly of knives, as Andrew tried to defend himself. The knives melted before they could even make contact, the liquid metal evaporating before it could even hit the ground. Mark took another step forward.\
"You can't keep this form up forever!" Andrew shouted, a slight quiver in his voice. The heat was already beginning to dissipate, Marks flames were dying down as well. He took another step forward.\
A pole pierced Mark's stomach, the steel persisting through the weakened heat. He took another step forward.\
"Is this good enough for you?" Andrew looked around frantically, as if searching for someone who wasn't there. "Get me the fuck out of here before this psycho kills me!"\
Mark now stood directly above Andrew, his fiery form looking down on him. Andrew let out one final laugh. "Wait a sec, you're not Mark, are you?"\
Mark did not even touch Andrew. He simply stuck out his hand, and let go. Fire gushed forth from his palm, it's roar filling the air. He let it go on for only a second, leaving behind a corpse that did not resemble a human in any distinguishable way.\
"Mark" turned around to face the lake. He took a step forward, the flames covering him wavering slightly. Mark collapsed, one arm still reaching for the lake.

---
"Your perseverance is outstanding."\
Nightmare stood over the charred corpse, the black crystal still intact. "The flames will have only made you stronger. We just need some time for you to recover, that's all."\
Nightmare faced Mark, who was currently lying face down in the dirt. The entire upper half of his body still burned, the smell of his flesh beginning to waft through the area. Nightmare straightened his tie and crouched down next to Mark, the flames licking his face. "Do you remember me? Is that why your fire burns so brightly? Is there a part of you that remembers your hatred?"\
The fire lashed out, attempting to burn the man. He stood up, taking a step as he slicked back his hair. "I knew this vessel was incredible, but this is something else." He began to walk back to Andrew, glancing back at Mark. "Impressive work, Solaris."\
Nightmare picked up Andrew burn wrist, pressing on the crystal. It began to sink deeper into what remained of Andrews skin, black tendrils working their was throughout his inner body. After a moment, Nightmare felt a weak pulse. He began to drag Andrew by his arm, towards the trees and into the darkness, when he felt the presence of another. He heard a brach snap nearby and whipped his head, trying to locate the source of the disturbance. Just then, he felt a sharp, stabbing sensation in his back. He looked down at his chest, to which he noticed a large piece of the Earth had come up from the ground behind and punctured him. No blood dripped from his flesh, but his new suit was ruined.\
"Where do you think you're going asshole?"\
Nightmare lifted up his head, the presence finally making itself known. Kneeling on the ground ahead of him, covered in sweat and out of breath, was Terra. No. Terra's *vessel*. Nightmare chuckled. "Not yet."\
Nightmare gripped the end of the stone, shattering it completely. He flicked his right wrist downwards, causing Vincent to sink downwards.\
"Fight me like a man you coward!" Vincent called out, his hands and legs slowly descending into the earth.\
"Oh, but I am no mere man." Nightmare responded, the shadows beginning to consume him and Andrew. He looked behind Vincent, glancing at the lake in the distance. "How interesting." he commented, before vanishing completely.\
Vincent looked down to see his hands no longer sinking, but simply sitting atop the grass, like they were never sinking to begin with.\
"Mark!" Vincent yelled out, forgetting the monster and rushing to save his friend.

---
Mark jolted up, the ice cold water sending a shock throughout his body. In front of him stood Vincent, bucket in hand, with a stressed look on his face. This felt oddly familiar.\
"What the fuck dude." Mark wiped the water off his face, moving his wet hair out of his eyes.\
"Dude, you started to burn my bed." Vincent responded.\
A sense a realization washed over Mark. Memories of the past 3 hours started to flood in, a sense of panic consuming him. He frantically ran his hands over his body, looking for any signs of damage. He tore off his shirt to reveal his crystal, it's familiar glow lighting up the room. *Holy shit*. The crystal itself was cracked, per usual, but something else had appeared. Scars surrounded the crystal, scars that Mark was all too familiar with. Scars that covered his fathers arms and back, their source being what eventually consumed him.\
Mark looked up at Vincent, who was now suspiciously hiding his hands behind his back. "What happened to me?" Mark asked.\
"I really couldn't tell ya," his friend replied. "I went lookin for you after I got back home, but your mom told me she hadn't seen you yet. So, I rushed over to Molly's and saw blood on the ground. I feared the worst." Mark almost didn't realize, but a small tear ran down the boys face. "I tried to search for you with my powers, but it was like something was concealing you, like someone didn't want you to be found. Then, a couple hours later, it happened." Vincent rubbed his hands together behind his back. "It's like my hands were stuck in the sun or something. Those crystals really fucking burned."\
Mark felt a pang of guilt. Had he done that? He truly couldn't remember anything beyond passing out, but could he had really burned Vincent from so far away?\
"And then, the feeling was just gone," Vincent continued, "and suddenly it was like I knew just where you were. Like an invisible compass was guiding me towards you. I got to the lake and there was fire everywhere, with you at the center and-"\
Mark curled his legs up to his chest. Only one thing was on his mind. "Molly," he croaked. "Oh my god, Molly." The image of her on the pole became the only thing he could see. What would he tell her parents? Would he just act like he didn't know what happened? Lie to everyones faces?\
Mark felt a hand on his shoulder, and looked up. Vincentt was looking at the window. "Yeah, about that."

---
Molly sat on the roof, her eyes staring at nothing. She wore one of Vincents sweaters, which was comically oversized and smelled like Doritos. She was freezing, so she didn't mind. A pair of birds flew past her, grabbing her attention. The two were playing with one another, landing on the roof besides her and bouncing around. A soft smile filled her face.\
"MOLLY!"\
The birds flew away, startled by the sound. Molly whipped her head around to see Mark, climbing up onto the roof. He reached the top, the two locking eyes for a moment, saying nothing. After what seemed like eternity, Mark stumbled over to Molly, before finally pulling her into an embrace. She hesitated, but eventually began to hug him back. "I thought I lost you forever," he sobbed into her shoulder, pulling her into a tighter hug. "I tried so hard to save you. I'm so sorry."\
Then something strange happened.\
Mark felt as though, for a moment, he was hugging nothing. He let go, and backed up slightly. His arms were soaked.\
"Shit," Molly mumbled under her breath, wiping away her tears. "I have to focus."\
"Do you want to tell him or should I?" Vincent said, now behind Mark. Mark looked between the two, his head bouncing back and forth like he was watching a ping pong tournament. He waved his arms, air-drying them. "What the fuck are you talking about?" he finally asked. Vincent just stood there with crossed arms.\
"Fine." Molly sighed, turning her body to face Mark. She lifted up the sweater slightly, revealing her stomach. Vincent shook his head. Mark was too stunned to speak. A small, ocean blue crystal sat slightly above her naval, a shimmer flowing through it like waves.\
"I'm a freak now, happy?" she said.\
Vincent grunted. "Welcome to the club."

Vincent sat around the dinner table with his family. His two sisters fought over something silly while his parents talked about their day. These family dinners didn't happen often, but when they did, there special feeling that filled Vincent, something he never felt elsewhere. He smiled.

---

Molly sat on her roof, swirling her finger around the new jewelry permanently engraved on her body. She avoided her parents and older sister as much as possible, mostly out of fear of revealing her secret. If she lost concentration for even a second, a piece of her could fall apart. She thought back to the lake, the little memories she had from her near death state. She thought of Mark and how he wept for her. She sighed, looking up at the stars, wondering what would come next.

---
Mark's mom laid passed out at the opposite end of the couch, her snoring overpowering the Tv's volume. Nara was also passed out, weighing down Marks chest as she slept. He held the photo of him and his father in his hand, his dads scared arms keeping a little Mark up on his hip. Mark flipped over the photo, reading his Mom's writing. "James and best friend <3".\
Mark didn't know what was next. There were so many questions. In just a month, his life had changed so much. He kissed Nara on her forehead. He didn't want to worry about that right now. In this moment, he was just a normal boy with his dog.
