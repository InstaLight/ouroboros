Vincent reeled back in pain, dropping the boy. He clutched his right hand, which felt as though thousands of watts ran though it, to see a recently formed crack in his crystal. He looked back at the boy, who was now standing, a large spike now protruding from his back. Slowly, hundreds of metal spikes begin protruding from the boy's flesh, the sight being as grotesque as one may imagine. Tears streaked the boy's face, but the smile persisted, his mind cried out for his help while his body acted, as if it was being pulled by some unknown force. Vincent winced, watching the boy's body tear itself apart. Out of all the innocent people that were sent after him, this was the youngest. Refusing to let his body be overwhelmed by his emotions, Vincent took a step back, sat down on the grassy floor, and began his meditation. He blocked out the danger that was currently ahead of him, and focused on everything else. The ants that walked passed his hands, the food they were carrying back to their colony. The birds rushing by, migrating before fall had fully arrived. The boy began to rush towards him, a human pincushion with spikes sharp enough to pierce even Vincent's rock hard flesh. Among the animalistic sounds coming out of the boy, Vincent heard a small voice. The boy, trapped in his own shell of a body, crying for his mother. Eyes remaining closed, Vincent pressed his palms deeper into the Earth, the ground around them beginning to tremble. Vincent opened his eyes to see a sight even he could not fully believe. Centimeters from his face rested a spike that protruded from the boy's fingertip, while the rest of his body, with the exception of his face, was fully entombed in rock. The boy, who was still conscious, screamed and tried trashed around, attempting to free himself from his new prison. Vincent got up close to his face, reassuring the boy, "Everything is going to be okay now, I got you."  
Vincent moved the boy's hair out of the way to reveal the shadow-like crystal he had spotted earlier. He reached out his left hand, attempting to touch the crystal, only to be met with a strange resistance, like when magnets repel one another. Vincent tried to push through the barrier, but it was no use, his hand shot back every time he got too close. The boy continued to struggle, his yells getting louder by the minute, Vincent's anxiety beginning to shoot through the roof. "This would not be a good look if someone came by right now," he thought to himself, "I gotta get this kid out of here."  
Just then, the boy stopped screaming, the yells being replaced with silent sobbing. He made eye contact with Vincent, as the crystal on his forehead began to shatter.  
"It's starting," he whispered.  
Vincent kneeled down, getting close to the boy's face. "What is?" he asked softly.  
"The Nightmare. It's show is about to begin."  
The crystal shattered completely, the boy's body going limp.  
"No no no no no, you're okay, you're okay," Vincent said in a panic, releasing the walls that trapped the boy. Blood covered his entire entire body, the spikes having torn up his flesh. Vincent found a pulse, though it was barely there. He picked up the boy, allowing some of the spikes to pierce him in order to do so, and began to run, shouting for someone, anyone, to come help.

---

Mark knew that if his mother could see him right now, she would be very disappointed. Here he was, middle of the day, walking down a strange, dark alleyway with some girl he met only moments ago. It sounds bad, but in his defense, imagine if you spent all day looking for something, you're tired, you're stressed, only for some random person to come up to you and be like "hey! I know where that thing you're looking for is!" You would follow them too.  
"I'm Daisy, by the way!" she shouted.  
"Oh, like the Mario character!" Mark replied.  
"Who?"  
"Nevermind."  
The two arrived at the end of the alley, the only light coming from the sun, struggling to break through the cloudy sky. "Can I ask you a personal question?" Daisy said, turning around, taking a step closer to Mark. His heartbeat quickened.  
"Sure, what's up?"  
"Do you have a girlfriend?"  
Mark's face turned beet red as his gaze shifted towards the ground, looking anywhere but Daisy's face.  
"Where did you say you saw the man in the suit again?" Mark changed the subject, feeling uncomfortable. Daisy pouted.  
"It was right around here. I saw it all happen you know. That poor girl, she was suffering for so long," she put her arms behind her back. "Every night, she would experience the most terrifying Nightmares. Visions of her and her family, their bodies hung like cow's meat. She heard a voice telling her it could all go away. So she listened. Next thing she knew, that man was holding her, whispering the most bizarre things in her ear."  
Mark grew tense, and he began to caress his newly acquired ring on his right hand.  
"Then, some brute came along and tried to kill her! He probably thought she died, he looked so pitiful." Daisy looked up at Mark, a smirk now formed. "He should've known some people are persistent."  
Daisy revealed her arms, which had now morphed into what appeared to be giant octopus tentacles, which lashed out and wrapped around Mark, pulling him closer.  
"I was serious about the girlfriend question, by the way." she said, pulling him in. "You're pretty cute."  
Mark rested his left hand on her tentacles. "Sorry, you're not my type." Daisy's eyes widened, as she spotted blood spilling down her arm. "Ignite."  
Daisy screamed out, as both of her tentacle-arms had been blown clean off, now writhing on the ground, engulfed in flames. She began laughing to herself, as she licked the blood sprouting from her wounds. "I like when my man has a little fire in him." Her arms began to regrow, sprouting out of her like little sprouts, before being fully formed. Mark gagged a little. Just as her arms were completely reformed, her hands began to morph, her left hand resembling a bear claw, and her right a pair of talons. Mark created some distance by hopping backwards, taking the time to plan his next moves. While claw based attacks will cut him and allow him to create more flames, there is also a high likelihood that he could pass out from blood loss again, and with Vincent at least thirty minutes away, he couldn't take that chance. Additionally, he would have to control the strength of his flames, so he could increase the amount of time he had to wield them before his wound healed completely. With this in mind, he allowed his blood to drip down his left arm, igniting it as it reached his elbow. He took up a defensive position, using his now ignited arm as a shield, waiting for his opponent to strike.  
She was in front of him before he could blink. Without him realizing, she had morphed her legs into that of a cheetah, allowing her to move at 113kph, though just for a moment. That moment was all she needed however, and her talons went for his throat, while her claw sliced through his stomach. It took everything in Mark to not set all of his blood ablaze in that moment. If he did, it would surely do enough harm to send Daisy scurrying, if not kill her. However, setting that much blood off would surely knock him out from the pain in seconds, and he couldn't risk that happening with no backup around. Using his already activated left arm, he grabbed her left arm, his flames enhancing his strength, preventing her from digging anymore into his neck, before calmly igniting the three wounds she had made on his neck, just enough to stop the bleeding. He didn't like what he had to do next. While he was able to heal his neck quickly and relatively pain free due to the size of the claw marks, he could not do the same with the wound on his stomach. He used his right foot to kick her leg, causing her to stumble backwards. He then slammed his left arm against his stomach, instantly cauterizing the wound. It hurt like hell, but not as much as if he ignited it instead. Daisy smiled and licked her lips.  
"You're even more impressive than he said you were," she slowly got back up on her feet.  
"Who the hell is he?" Mark yelled, growing impatient with her flirty remarks.  
She giggled. "Salvation."  
From her back, two gigantic monarch butterfly wings appeared, as she rocketed off from the ground, winking at Mark as she flew away.  
"What the hell is going on down here?" A police officer yelled down the alleyway. Mark sprinted off down another path, grabbing his phone to call Vincent.

---

Mark laid on Vincent's bed, as Vincent stood over him holding a recently heated knife.  
"Are you sure you want ME to do this?" Vincent asked, unsure of the job he was assigned.  
"I'm too much of a baby to do it on myself, lets just get it over with."  
"Dude you cauterized it in the first place."  
"Just cut the fucking wound dude."  
Vincent went through each wound, slowly cutting with the hot blade, Mark softly igniting as he went. Now that Mark was in a calm environment and was able to focus, he had much more control over the intensity of his flames, allowing him to simply heal instead of setting himself on fire. Still hurt though.  
"Sooooooooo," Vincent said, sweat beading down his face as he cut. "Should we talk about both of the cryptic ass messages we heard today?"  
"What the hell is 'salvation' supposed to mean," Mark's face was buried in his hands as he tried to distract himself from the pain.  
"You think all that shit she said about the nightmares was true? Do you think that could mean something?"  
"Hell if I know." Vincent stopped for a moment. "Say, have you had any weird dreams since you got your crystal?"  
Mark thought back to a few days ago, after his first real fight with Andrew, and that weird dream with the orb and somebody watching him. "Yeah, last week. It was the most vivid dream I've ever experienced."  
"Had something like that happen to me once, only it wasn't a nightmare, and I was surrounded by a bunch of hot chicks and dudes." Vincent's hand slipped, causing the knife to go deeper into Mark then intended. Mark yelled. Vincent apologized profusely before resuming the operation.  
"Hey, how'd my ring work out!" Vincent said nervously, trying to keep Mark calm. "That was a great idea by the way, having a little hidden blade so that you can activate your powers in a pinch!  
"It was great, I can demonstrate right now if you want."  
"Anyways, some part of me is glad that that Daisy girl is still alive," Vincent said, Mark trying his hardest not to set him on fire after what just happened. "I remember her. I found her crying down this ally and I was just checking up on her. Next thing I know, boom, she's got some crazy cow horns and is trying to impale me!"  
"What about that kid that tried to get you today, were you able to get him help?"  
Vincent sighed. "I had to carry him all the way to the closest hospital myself. Had to get out of there before any cops showed up, but he still had a pulse thankfully. Or maybe not thankfully. Those spikes did a number on him, I'm not sure if it's possible to even remove them. I guess I'm just relieved I was able to hold back enough this time. It makes me proud of how far I've come."  
"You said he mentioned something about a show beginning? I don't like the sound of that."  
Vincent lifted the blade, the operation now complete. He sat down and began to chug a bottle of water. "With our luck, it means that things are about to get a lot worse." He smacked his hands on his legs and stood up, making a big deal out of stretching and yawning. "In the meantime, I have 3 exams tomorrow and I am exhausted. We could become famous super heroes but at the end of the day I still need to pass calc if I want to graduate, so some creepy shadow dude can wait."
