*I should've never gotten out of bed this morning,* Mark though to himself, looking out the cars window. In the backseat with him was his best friend / current enemy Molly, who at the current point in time was acting as though he did not exist. His mother, blissfully unaware of the two's current dislike of one another, was singing along cheerfully as she drove them to their first day of high school. Mark, as always, was completely oblivious as to why him and Molly weren't talking to one another. Just last week, they were biking to Johnsons for ice cream to celebrate the end of summer, and now she had some weird look of disgust whenever she looked at him. *Is she embarrassed to show up in the same car as me?* he wondered, *It's not my fault her parents couldn't drive her*. After what seemed like hours, the pair finally pulled into their new school. Molly finally spoke, giving the most cheerful "Thank you for the ride!" Mark had ever heard before promptly slamming the door. "Todays a big day for both of you, just try and stick together and you'll both figure it out." Mark's mom said, looking back at him. He couldn't even respond, giving his mom a weak smile as he sulked out of the car on onto the pavement. He basically had to jog to catch up to Molly, who turned around and stared him down before he could even open his mouth. "Listen," she began, "we can't be seen together and that's the honest truth. Veronica told me that if we're seen walking around together, there could be 'implications', and I'm trying to avoid any drama. This school is a fresh start for the both of us, and I won't let you and your clingy self ruin it for me!" 

The next 8 hours were some of the worst of Mark's entire life. Somehow, he got lost on his way to his first class, and his second, and his third. He was just lost in general honestly. By lunch time, Molly had already made a new gaggle of friends, while Mark kind of just awkwardly walked around in circles looking for somewhere to sit and have a moment to rest. After about 10 minutes of searching, he gave up and just went and waited in his next classes room. He felt like everywhere he went, people were watching him, nitpicking his every move. These of course, are irrational thoughts, but our protagonist is a moody 14 year old boy, therefore everything he does is irrational. By the time the clock hit 2:50, Mark was just about ready to go home, go into his room, and never come out again. Except that wouldn't be happening for at least another three hours. See, just before the school year started, Mark's mom thought it would be a good idea for him to join a team! Something to get him out there, make some new friends and have some fun! Mark was no stranger to sports, being forced to try every possible one growing up, but he was never really that good at any of them. So when the time came to pick a team, he chose the sport that seemed the easiest: cross country. It's just running, how hard could it be? Mark trudged his way to the locker room, which was already packed, and found his way to a locker that had a piece of painters tape with his name on it stuck to the front. He quickly got changed and started frantically looking for the "team room" that he was told to report to in an email he received a few days prior. *There's no way I'm lost again* he sighed. He had tried almost every room in the locker room, finding literally everything but the one he was supposed to be in. It was finally down to two doors, a 50/50 chance. Mark picked wrong. Opening the first door revealed a room a dark room, illuminated only by the sunlight through one tiny window, with four boys in the center. Two of the boys were standing, chucking with one another, passing a tiny joint between them. The third was in a chair, with what looked like tears in his eyes, and a towel tied around his mouth. The fourth and final boy, who was about two years older than Mark, was kneeling down, holding a knife. In a state of panic, Mark quickly shut the door and practically bursted through the second door, which finally led him to the fated "team room", which was currently packed with runners. His mind began racing, unsure if what he just saw was real or his mind playing tricks on him. *There's no way they saw me right?* his thoughts were everywhere, *Should I tell someone?* Next thing he knew, he was out on the trail at a nearby park, running by himself. The run, despite the horrible day and potentially gruesome scene he just witnesses, was quite relaxing. It allowed him to slow down his mind, and think of his next steps. He would go to his coach and tell him everything he saw. Of course, nothing today has gone Mark's way, so why would it start now. He noticed the sound of footsteps coming up directly behind him, as a boy appeared and started jogging next to Mark. His eyes went wide. It was the same boy who had been stuck in the chair. With dead eyes, the boy introduced himself, "Hey there, my names Tim, it's nice to meet you." Mark's brain was unable to process in time, so he responded with "I'm Mark." Tim turned his head and looked behind him, giving a weak thumbs up to someone behind them. 

Despite his lack of practice, Mark was fast. Unfortunately for him, as he would find out later, the three boys chasing him were all top runners on the team. Mark sprinted up the side of a hill as fast as he could before feeling a pair of hands grab the back of his shirt and shove him to the ground. His face smashed into a rock, causing blood to gush from his nose. He was flipped onto his back, and was greeted with a malicious grin. "Tell me," the boy said, pulling out a blade from his shorts, "what exactly did you see back there?" Now seeing his attacker in better lighting, Mark was a little surprised. If it wasn't for the big ass switch-blade and evil goons around him, Mark probably would've believe this guy to be a pretty upstanding young man. He looked like he could've been some kind of magazine model, dark skin, short, black curly hair and a chiseled frame. Mark, on the other hand, was built like a twig. There was only one possible outcome for this fight, especially with a knife involved. "Hurry up Andy," one of the goons said in a strained voice "we can't be gone for too long." "I'll take as long as I want! Tell me to hurry up again and you're switching places with this freshman." He hissed, looking back at his groupies. Mark seized the opportunity to kick the bully right in his special spot, in an attempt to squirm free. This, however, only angered Andrew. As Mark tried to kick away, Andrew plugged his knife directly into Mark's side. "HOLY SHIT," one of the duo shouted, as the other started pacing back and forth. Mark's vision began to spin, and he suddenly became unable to focus. Andrew's eyes were all over the place, not even realizing what he had just done. He pulled the blade from the side of Mark's abdomen, causing blood to spill all over the leaf covered floor of the forrest. Andrew fell backwards, looking at the blood on his hands. One of his friends ran off, while the other frantically looked around for spectators. "We have to get out of here man, we can come up with a story or something later but we can't be anywhere near here." he pleaded with Andrew. "We can't let him live now," Andrew whispered under his breathe, "no one can find out." Mark was back on his stomach, using all of his strength to stand up. *I have to get away, I have to get to safety.* There was nowhere to go. He finally made it to the top of the hill, only to be greeted with a steep drop off. Falling from that distance would kill anyone. He started to turn around, in an attempt to run past his attackers, but it was too late. Andrew was already behind him, and with a single shove, Mark was over the edge.

Mark did not lead an extraordinary life. He wasn't the best at anything. He got average grades in school. He didn't even have that many friends. As he fell, he wondered if anything would be different if he lived. Would his existence amount to anything? *Probably not* he wondered. Even if he did live past that fateful day, would he have any kind of impact on the world? There was one thought that stuck out in his mind in his final moments, one that overpowered all the negativity that was streaming through his consciousness: *I wish I could see her again*. Thud.

His body laid at the bottom of a trench, bloodied and mangled. The blood seeping out of him felt warm, but his body was slowly turning cold. He couldn't move anything, he couldn't see anything, he could only feel this agonizing pain in his final moments. He fell on some kind of rock which was digging into his back, making his final breathes that much more painful, just his luck. *I hope that somebody finds my body and brings that bastard to justice*, he began to pray. *He'll pay for this, I know he will*. His blood began to boil as anger and rage invaded his final thoughts. His vision turned red. The rock in his back began to dig. Deeper. and deeper. Until it felt like it was burrowing directly through him. *Wait. What?* Something wasn't right. His pain increased, his blood feeling like it was actually boiling him alive. His body finally gave in, allowing him peace as he passed out from the pain.

Mark awoke to the world around him on fire. First, he heard the flames roar, then he smelled the burning wood. When he opened his eyes, the entire forest around him was on fire. Temporarily forgetting the whole ordeal he bolted upwards, screaming "HOLY SHIT I'M GONNA DIE." He snapped back to reality, as memories of the past 24-hours began flooding in. *How am I alive right now? Is this hell?* For the second time today, he began sprinting like his life depended on it, finally making it to a clearing by the parking lot where he started his run. In his moment of rest, he began examining himself for his injuries, which to his astonishment were completely nonexistent. If it weren't for the cut shirt and huge blood stains, you would think he went for a walk in the park. He didn't even get any burns from the flames. He lifted up his shirt to look closer, and his eye's widened. Planted dead center in his chest laid a crimson crystal, which was currently emitting a soft glow. 

*What. the. fuck*.
