Tim was never what you'd consider a "popular kid" in school. He had some friends growing up, but never a best friend. That was all going to change. After spending a week with Mark, Tim knew that he had finally found someone special. A BFF who he could talk to about anything, who he wanted to spend all his time with. On Friday, when Tim came out of the bathroom, Mark was nowhere to be found! He panicked, his mind going towards the worst. What if his best friend got attacked by some crazy animal? Tim began searching, yelling out Mark's name. Finally, Tim saw a random guy come out of the forest, carrying an unconscious Mark.  
"What are you doing with him?" Tim shouted, marching towards the mystery man while trying to disguise the fact that he was terrified.  
The man glared at Tim, before a sense of realization washed over his face. "You're Tim, right? I've seen you sitting with Mark before at lunch. I'm Vincent, it's nice to meet you."  
"You didn't answer my question," Tim responded, slowly gaining confidence, "What are you doing with my friend?"  
"Calm down, I found him passed out in the woods and was taking him to get some rest. Would you be able to tell your coach about this? It would mean a lot. Thanks Tim!"  
Before Tim could even respond, Vincent just kept walking, heading down the trail with Mark in hand.  
The first weekend of the school year was agonizing. Two whole days without seeing his new bestie? The horrors! Tim thought about asking Mark to come over to his house, but he decided against it, he didn't want to look like some kind of creep! When Monday finally rolled around, Tim anxiously waited until lunch, tapping his leg in anticipation throughout his classes. When that fateful bell rang, Tim rushed (speed-walked), down the halls and into the cafeteria. His eyes scanned the room only to see-

---

"I'm telling you dude, this manga's going to be huge," Vincent said, shoving his lunch into his face. "It's a high school of super heroes, what's cooler than that?"  
Mark nodded along in agreement, with a big smile on his face. After 7 days, he was finally experiencing a calm day of high school. Mark looked up to see Tim, eyes wide, standing behind Vincent.  
"Hey Tim, come take a seat!" Mark called out.  
Tim blinked like he was snapped out of a trance and sat down next to Vincent, still side-eyeing him. Vincent, completely oblivious to Tim's obvious dislike of him, put his arm around the boy and asked "So Timmy, what's your favorite anime?"  
As lunch went on, Tim began to warm up to Vincent, and slowly the trio had a banter going. By the end of the day, the three of them had exchanged contact info and had created a group chat. For the first time in a while, Mark felt happy. He didn't even mind Molly ignoring him this time! After practice, instead of going home like usual, Mark ended up at Vincent's doorstep. Mark rang the doorbell, and was greeted by a small girl, who looked like she was about to throw up.  
"You smell weird," she said, before proceeding to throw up all over Mark's shirt. A second later, Vincent appeared in the doorway, doubled over in laughter.  
"I am so sorry," he said, gasping for air, "that was my little sister Rachel, she's been home sick for the past day and thought it would be a great idea to answer the door. Come in, I'll get you a fresh shirt."  
Mark changed, putting on an oversized Metallica t-shirt, before proposing an idea to Vincent.  
"I want you to train me," Mark said. Vincent looked like he was about to cry from laughing again.  
"Train you? What does that even mean, how would that even work?"  
"You clearly have some control over your powers without exerting yourself too much, and I need you to walk me through how you got to this point. I mean, you've had them for a year, you must have some tips."  
Vincent scratched the back of his head. "I mean sure, but I don't know how they would apply to you. Our powers aren't similar and they affect our bodies in different ways, so I'm not sure how much they'll help out."  
Mark pondered. Vincent had a point, from what he had told Mark about his powers, there were no similarities. Nothing besides-  
"The crystals!" Mark shouted. "So far I've only been able to use my powers subconsciously, I've never been able to do it on command!"  
A smile spread across Vincent's face. "We can work on that."

---

"Not to pry, " Mark started as the pair walked into Vincent's backyard, "but where are your parents?"  
Vincent chuckled as he dug around in a box, "Yeah my folks are pretty busy. They both work two jobs, so we really only see them super late or super early during the week. My dad gets weekends off though, so that's nice." Vincent pulled out two yoga mats from the box. Mark groaned as obnoxiously as possible.  
"There is no way you expect me to do yoga." He protested.  
"Don't bash it till you've tried it." Vincent shot back. "Now sit down and shut up."  
Mark, feeling like a total idiot, sat down on the mat and crossed his legs, closing his eyes just as Vincent was. "Now, take in a deep breath, and just listen."  
The backyard wasn't too big, but it was surrounded by a line of trees, their leaves rustling in the soft wind.  
"The first time I did this, I was amazed at all the beautiful things I could hear," Vincent explained. "I'm not sure if it's due to my powers or what, but it's like I can hear everything that's connected to the earth, like the trees themselves are singing a sweet song. I can hear even the smallest of insects crawling around, searching for something to eat. I like to come out here and just immerse myself in nature. It makes me feel like I'm apart of something amazing."  
Mark sighed. "I don't know, I can't hear any of that."  
"Like I said, it could just be the crystal enhancing something in me, but I still think you'll get something out of it."  
The two sat there for ten minutes, in total silence. The longer they sat there, the more aware Mark became. His experience was different from Vincent's however. Instead of hearing the motions of individual life, he instead heard what he thought of as a thumping sound. The sound was coming from everywhere. Some were loud, some were soft, some were quick like a jackhammer, while others were slow, like someone was softly tapping on a drum. Mark became lost in the sounds, losing track of time, before he was snapped back to reality by Vincent, who was shaking him.  
"Damn dude, you were really into it," Vincent said. "I was about to smack you out of it."  
Mark shook his head, snapping out of his meditation, before telling Vincent about what he had heard.  
Vincent put his hand on his chin and began stroking an invisible beard. "Innnnnnteresting," he said, talking like he was an old detective. "Well my good chap, I think that's enough for today, you better hurry along home now."  
Mark jolted up, confused by the sudden change in mood. "We haven't even talked about controlling my power's, why are you telling me to go home?"  
"I'm the teacher," Vincent responded, still speaking in a terrible accent, "and you are the student. Do not question my teachings! Now shoo, you need your rest my boy, for tomorrow is a new day!" Vincent then began actively shooing Mark away, to which Mark rudely responded with a middle finger before heading home.

---

By day four of meditation, Mark was really getting into the flow. Their sessions were getting longer every day, and Mark began to feel more in touch with the world around him. On day two, he could control how much or how little thumping he wanted to hear. On day three, he could pick out distinct thumps, and focus on them separately. By today, he had finally deduced the source of the sound.  
"It's heartbeats." he stated calmly, eyes still shut. "I can sense living creatures' heartbeats. It can feel their life force, the blood flowing through their bodies." Mark opened his eyes to see Vincent grinning ear to ear.  
"Bang," Vincent shot up two finger guns, "You're finally in touch with nature. A little more creepy than my thing but we'll go with it. Now then, we can finally focus on control. Tell me Mr. Cassidy, what did your heart want when you first activated your abilities?"  
Mark (who never told Vincent his last name but whatever), sat confused. "What did my heart want?"  
"Yeah, y'know like what did you feel! Take me for example, I wanted to protect that girl more than anything. My heart wanted to save someone who I didn't even know. That sense of protection is what allows me to use my abilities. So, what did you want?"  
Mark thought back to the moment when he first attained the crystal, how angry he was with everything, how he wanted nothing more than revenge.  
"I guess I just felt angry. My heart felt rage."  
"Well aren't you a little ball of joy and wonder. Ok ok, so we have our emotional trigger, and based on what I saw in the forest, your physical trigger is drawing blood. So I guess there's only one way forward." Vincent swiftly bent down, touching a rock on the ground, and using his abilities gave it a sharp edge. He grabbed Mark's left palm and quickly sliced it open. Mark recoiled in pain. Before Mark had a chance to even get angry, Vincent got up in his face and screamed "AHHHHHHHHH." Mark wasn't even mad anymore, just very confused. He needed better friends.  
"I use that one on my sisters when they won't stop complaining, it surprises them so much they just shut up. The yelling, not the cutting with a dirty rock. Quickly now, sit on that yoga mat and focus!"  
"You're an asshole." Mark muttered under his breath before sitting back down. He closed his eyes, just as he had for the past few days, and began to sink deep into thought.  
"Focus on your own breathing, your own 'thumping'," Vincent whispered into his ear. "Feel the blood pump through your heart, down your arm and out through the wound."  
Mark could feel it, feel each individual blood cell making its way throughout his whole body. He could hear his own heartbeat though it was playing through a speaker at maximum volume. He drowned out all the other noise, and focused on the blood that was escaping from the cut. He could sense it so vividly, it felt as if even the blood that stained the rock was still connected to him. When it dripped onto the grass at his feet, he could feel it flow down and into the dirt.  
"Now, very slowly, imagine that you're raising the temperature on a stove. Slowly turn the nob."  
Mark did as Vincent said, turning the imaginary nob. He opened his eyes to reveal a small flame dancing around in his hand, the wound slowly closing. The fire was different then before, it wasn't as intense and there wasn't any sort of blast when it ignited. It felt like it was still part of him. The flame died quickly, and the evidence of his injury was gone. Vincent began jumping for joy around the backyard, shouting "I AM THE GREATEST TEACHER OF ALL TIME!"

---

Mark walked back home, the sun almost fully set. He kept glancing at his palm, smiling at the progress he made. Even without meditating, he still noticed a distant thumping. Every time he passed someone, though it was faint, he could hear their heart beat. Something about that sense of connection to life warmed him. Mark was only two minutes away from his house when he spotted him. He felt no thumping. No sign of life. If Mark didn't know any better, he would've thought it was just some poor guy walking home from a long day at work. He smiled at Mark. Not a sinister smile, but something different. Something unnerving.  
It was the man in the suit.
