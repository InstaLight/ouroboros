It was now mid October, and fall was finally in full effect. Mark gazed out the window, watching the tree's newly changed orange leaves fall off, one by one, creating a pile around its base. Mark adored the fall, when the sky turns grey for days and the occasional fog sets in. This time of year always brought him a sense of comfort, especially when in the middle of a boring English class, as the teacher droned on and on about greek mythology. Mark's eyes began to scan around the room as he observed what his fellow classmates were doing. At first glance, it appeared that only Tim was paying attention, diligently taking notes on everything that was being said, while everyone else was either asleep or playing games on their laptops. Well, almost everyone. Marks eyes locked with a girl that he had never noticed before, though she had probably always been there. She had long auburn hair, similar to the leaves he had been watching just a moment before, along with thick black glasses that made her terracotta eyes pop. The girl looked away, as she pretended like she was taking notes.\
"Mr. McQuaid," the teacher raised his voice, brining Mark's attention front and center. Since you *clearly* don't have to pay attention to this lecture and would rather oogle your fellow classmates, perhaps you could teach the class the meaning of the ***Ouroboros***?"\
"Uhhhhhh"\
Everyone turned to look at Mark, including Tim who looked like he was about to throttle him. Mark, in fact, had no clue what that thing was, nor did he really care. His phone began to blow up, a feeling of relief washing over him. "I uhhhh gotta go pee." He said, walking out of class before the teacher could give him permission. As he closed the door behind him, he heard Tim yell "I know the answer!"

---

"Are we sure it's them?" Mark asked, pulling on his helmet.\
Vincent followed suit, pulling down his own mask. "Positive."\
Just moments before, the pair had recieved an alert to their custom app, which Vincent was currently dubbing *"Find my Villain"*. You could probably guess what Mark thought of the name. It had been just about three weeks since they had last heard of those [[Chapter 9|two bank robbers]] that had kicked their asses and gotten away, and today those bastards decided to finally show up again. The two hopped in Vincent's car right away and sped down to Center City, the robbery still in progress. They observed the scene from a nearby rooftop, waiting for an optimal moment to strike. Mark grimaced at the scene. This wasn't just a run of the mill heist like before. They were putting on a performance, a grand show. Five people were stationed in front of the bank, a mix of tellers and customers, being used as hostages. The two robbers stood nearby, the one laughing and having a grand old time taunting police, while the other looked like he wanted to be anywhere but there. The two men were finally identified, two brothers named Alexander and Ivan Federnov. According to the news, Alexander, the younger of the two, was a member of the Russian Army, who had arrived in the United States recently in search of his older brother, Ivan, who had moved to get away from his family and pursue academics.\
"Won't anyone come and help these poor innocent people?" Alexander shouted, sprawling out his arms like a showman would during a performance. "Where are all the daring heroes? Surely they don't want these people to wind up dead, do they?" Behind him, one of the hostages began to wail. Alexander spun around and punched her, knocking the woman unconsious. "Stupid bitch." he mumbled. Mark began to tremble with anger, his body temperature starting to rise.\
"Stay calm," Vincent said, sensing Marks rage. He was currently crouched on th edge of the building, his palms touching the rooftop. "if we stick to the plan, we'll be in and out."\
Mark nodded, using his ring to make incisions through the center of his palms, allowing blood to flow freely. "You ready?" he asked his partner.\
Vincents eyes snapped open as he gripped the edge of the roof. "First one to knock their guy out gets $10!" he shouted, before lauching himself off the building, towards the center of the crowd. "I DIDNT AGREE TO THAT!" Mark shouted after him.

---
Ivan watched the ground, trying his best to block out all of the surrounding noise. Ivan himself was not a remarkable man. Sure, he was labled as the "smart" kid in all of his classes, but smarts can only get you so far. His life, for the most part, was quite ordinary. He worked an unremarkable nine-to-five as an accountant, making a decent paycheck, and would start and end every day at his mostly empty apartment. He once had dreams of falling in love, maybe even starting a family, but he felt that ideal life slipping away every day. The only girl he felt comfortable talking to was his coworker Susie, who was currently engaged to her highschool sweetheart. Nothing interesting never happened to Ivan, at least, not until *he* showed up. One night after another average day of work, Ivan returned home to his appartment only to find his little brother sitting in his kitchen, eating his leftover *Panda Express*.\
"Alexander?" Ivan said, his voice shaking. He dropped his bag to the floor without even realizing it.\
"IVY!" the man shouted. Ivan didn't even have to go near him to smell the alcohol on his breath. "You're one hard son of a bitch to find, you know that?" he slurred his words. Ivan wanted more then anything to reach into his pocket and call 911, but he was too frozen with fear to do anything. "Listen Ivy," Alexander, stumbled over a chair, making his way towards his terrified older brother. "I have a way for us to make some serious dough, but I need your brains to help me pull it off.\
Alexander would proceed to go on a long rant about how he dreamed of a prophecy from a man in a black suit, which Ivan believe to be utter nonsense, until Ivan himself was a recipient of those same dreams. The two would eventually awaken to powers, which Alexander declared as a gift from God himself. Fast forward a month later, and here Ivan was, going along with his psychotic brothers plans. Ivan's powers were far from remarkable, secreting a liquid that made anything that tried to touch him slide off, but Alexander kept telling him he had potential. Ivan continued to watch the ground, refusing to look at any of the hostages, or the growing crowd around them, when he suddenly felt a rumble. "Alexander," he called out to his brother. "I think-"\
Concrete suddenly sprung up from below, encasing the mans legs and pulling him slightly downwards. A cloud of smoke surrounded the brothers, their vision obscured. Alexander leaped, the floor unable to find anything to hold onto. Ivan watched as Alexanders eyes lit up; he looked just like a kid on Christmas. The boy in the oni mask appeared, cracking his knuckles as he stepped towards Ivan, who was trying to slip himself free from his earthly shackles. Alexander landed on the ground and began to rush towards the Oni, fists clenched. From his right, he felt a sudden wave of heat propelling towards him. He quickly slid to his knees, narrowly avoiding a flaming palm. "Nice of you to join us, flame boy!" Alexander yelled, the boy in the space helmet stepping out from the smoke.\
"If you ask nicely, we'll go easy on you." the boy said from behind the helmet.\
"Where's the fun in that?" Alexander asked, quickly rising back onto his feet.\
Ivan slipped his left leg out of the concrete, his body dripping with the mystery liquid. Just as his right began to slide out, he felt a hand on his shoulder. "Sorry man, but we gotta keep you locked up." the Oni said, before kneeling down and pressing his palms against the ground. Ivans left leg became encased again, and he felt the dirt beneath him begin to drag him down.\
"Two on one doesn't seem fair gents," Alexander yelled at the boys. "Not for a normal man, at least."\
The Oni once again slammed his hands into the ground, grunting with exertion. Slowly, stone walls began to rise around the three of them, beginning to trap them in a dome. Alexander sprinted towards the shortest of the stone walls, attempting to jump it before it closed him in, when he felt his entire lower body begin. The flame boy had not struck, but had simply dripped blood on Alexanders waist, causing causing everything below to briefly go up in flames.. Alexander flinched, the fire causing him to feel pain for the first time in many months. "You bitches choreographed this didn't you!" he mocked.\
"What a bunch of pussy's."

---
He wasn't wrong, Mark and Vincent had been planning for this since their first encounter. After their hilariously bad matchups last time, the two developed a strategy on how to deal with the brothers abilities. Ivan was easy, he had no offensive capabilities, purely defensive, so the easiest solution was to catch him by surprise. They knew Alexander, on the other hand, would be a little trickier. Due to his force absorption, the two couldn't just go at him guns blazing expecting to win. Instead, they decided to rely purely on Mark's flames, not the offensive enhancements that came with them. If Mark smeared a small amount of blood on any part of Alexanders body, the resulting flames would be strong enough to burn him, stunning him temporarily, but weak enough that they would dissipate after a moment, ensuring they didn't get too out of control. Using this method, the two now had the brothers each trapped in their own stone prisons of Vincent's design (he made it up on the fly). What resulted was a pissed off Russian and his mute older brother, both of which were now completely immobile.\
Vincent was already rushing over to the nearby press, shaking with excitement, when Mark felt a buzz in his pocket. He peered down at his phone, slightly lifting it from his pocket, and saw a text from Molly. He tapped on the notification to reveal an invitation from Molly, who invited him back over her house to talk, to which he responded with a verbal sigh. It had been two weeks since the two had even seen each other, and he really didn't want to hear what she had to say. Unfortunately, Mark is too nice for his own good, and responded anyways. "I'll be over in a few." he texted, dropping his phone back in his pocket as he walked over to join Vincent.\
"So you see," he heard his friend say, "we're simply just a pair of good samaritans! We don't need money or praise, we just want to help others!" Vincent leaned into the reporter, putting his hand up. "Though some good press never hurt anyone."\
The reporter flashed a cheap smile, now looking at Mark. "Everyone had been wondering, do you two have a name for yourselves? There's been heated debates on the subject, and I'd love to get an exclusive scoop!" she asked, shoving her microphone into Marks helmet.\
"Uhhhhhhhhh" he hadn't anticipated this question directly. Thankfully, Vincent had, as he snatched the mic out of the reporters hand.\
"Hey dude, I gotta head out, you go this right?" Mark whispered.\
"Don't worry man, I got this." Vincent was winking under his mask. Mark, for some reason, did not have faith in his bestie.\
Vincent faced the reporter again, and put a dramatic flare into his voice. "What a brilliant question! Being heroes of high status, we of course had to come up with names that fit our fame! That is why, today, I am proud to announce, that from this day forth, me and my partner will be known as-"\
Mark was too far away to hear the rest, as he began to jog towards home.

---

Mark stood out front of Molly's house, his left leg bouncing up and down. He had texted her that he arrived, but got no response, leading him to doubt if he should even go inside. There were no cars in her driveway, meaning her parents were probably still at work. Maybe she was taking a nap, maybe she-\
His phone buzzed, a response from her populating his screen. "Come in idiot." it read.\
His face turned red as he began marching towards her door, shoving his phone back in his pocket. He knocked, the door swingingly slightly open at his light tap.\
Mark put his hands behind his back, slicing his palm with the ring, concealing the blood in his now closed fist. He stepped in, the floorboards creaking as he walked across them.\
"Molly?" he shouted, his voice echoing through the hallways. "Is everything alright, the door was already-"
There was a crack. Time slowed to a halt, his brain trying it's hardest to process what had just happened. His vision went black, and he felt himself collapse onto the ground. Then, the pain set in. His brain slammed against his skull, sending unbearable jolts throughout his whole body. He tried to suck in air, gasping like a fish out of water. He couldn't move, he couldn't see, and a persistent ringing had found it's way into his ears. His vision began to return, though everything was still blurry. A red streak ran down his eyes, the familiar crimson hue allowing him to discern what had just happened. A metal baseball bat dinged on the ground in front of him. It's side dented and covered in blood. A figure walked into his view, bending down to make eye contact. He smiled at Mark.
"Amldiew" Mark mumbled, unable to form the name. He tried to ignite, but not even a spark emerged. The pain was taking over, and he was a second away from passing out. The boy moved his hand over Mark's eyelids, closing them for him. Mark felt warm breath by his ear, as the boy whispered to him. "Go to sleep Mark. You have to rest now, the big party is in an hour! I can't wait for you to see your present."
