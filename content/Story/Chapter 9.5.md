## POWERS

- When exposed to air, Mark's blood can be, how he says, "ignited". Upon ignition, the exposed blood bursts into flame, often accompanied by a concussive blast. We will be testing the range of ignition, seeing how far away Mark can be from the blood he's trying to ignite, and if it affects its properties in any way.
    
- Upon ignition, Mark's flames stick to his blood, giving a slight healing factor, as well as an increase in strength to the affected areas. From what we can tell, Mark's healing factor is relatively weak. While it can close wounds and stop active bleeding, it appears that he is unable to replenish the blood he lost, meaning that he can pass out or even die from blood loss. Additionally, we are unsure if conditions such as sickness or internal bleeding can be healed by the flames.
    

## EXPERIMENTS

### Experiment #1: Ignition Range

- To perform this test, Mark left a few drops of blood on a plate in my backyard. After several attempts, we've determined two things:
    

- 1st: Mark's ignition range is ~60 feet. We are currently unsure if this range can be increased in any way, we'll come back to this experiment at a later date
    
- 2nd: As I hypothesized, the further Mark is from his blood, the weaker the ignition. At maximum range, we saw a significant decrease in both flame intensity and concussive power. The flames were so weak in fact, that they had gone out before we were able to walk back to the plate.
    

### Experiment #2: Blood Age

- I have to give credit to Mark for this one, I would have never considered it. Mark hypothesized that the age, or maybe even dryness of the blood, could affect its properties in any way. To test this, we had Mark put samples of blood on 8 different plates, 7 of the plates were to be ignited at the end of each day, for a week straight, while the last one we dried out with a hair dryer. The following are our findings:
    

- Dried Blood: Incredible! When dried, the strength and intensity of Mark's flames are significantly increased! The flames got so high that we started to panic, worried that one of the neighbors might see. I'm not sure how effective this is in a combat scenario, but I'm sure we'll find some uses
    
- Aged blood: After performing the dried blood test, I was pretty positive that we were going to experience the same thing happen with the aged blood, due to it naturally drying out, but I was astonished! Mark's blood is like a fine wine, it only gets better with age! Not only did the properties of dry blood apply, but we made two other incredible findings. First, we discovered that the concussive blast was exponentially increased with each passing day, to the point where we almost got knocked on our asses on day 7! What's even more fascinating is that it appears that old blood somehow is able to return to Mark's blood stream?? We first noticed this clearly on day 4, where I had spotted a small amount of blood rush at Mark, hitting his crystal and being absorbed. On day 7, it appeared as though 60% of the blood Mark left behind on the plate was returned to him, while the other 40% burned up like normal. I wonder if we could make a sort of blood bomb? I'll mention the idea to him when he's no longer mad at me for having him do so many experiments
    

### Experiment #3: Flight!

- This one was short lived, I think Marks is avoiding me now. I proposed that he could use his feet as rockets, using the blood to fly. He made a thin cut in the middle of his foot, from his heel to his toes, and tried to blast off. It did not go well. He set his pants on fire. I owe him $30 now. Maybe if he makes multiple, smaller cuts it'll work better! We'll come back to this one later.
    

### Experiment #4: Viscosity

- This is merely a hypothesis, we both agreed it was too dangerous to do any real testing. Up until this point, Mark has been getting hit in pretty non-vital areas. I mean he still could've died don't get me wrong, but no major organs or blood vessels have been hit. If say a normal person cut their hand, blood would be gushing depending on how deep the cut, but it wouldn't be anything crazy. I wonder, if an area where more pressure is built up, such as the aorta, is hit, will his blood take on different properties when ignited? Will it just be an increase in intensity and self-benefiting factors? Will it take on magma like properties? I hope we don't ever find out, but if we do, I'll come back and update this part.
    

### Experiment #5: Crystal on Crystal.

- Due to Mark being upset with me already, this is going to be our final experiment (for now ;p). This test was simple: how will our crystals react when coming in contact with one another? We both know from personal experiences that these crystals have to have some sort of intelligence, I mean how else would they have attached themselves to us in the first place. When I bring my two palms together, it's kind of like a magnet, where they just stick to one another, and it feels kinda nice. So, for this experiment, I tried placing my palm on Mark's crystal. Uhm. I didn't like that. First, it was like magnet repulsion, my hand was kind of flinging back. The more I tried to fight it, the more it fought against me. I was able to touch it perfectly fine with my finger tips (I don't recommend doing this for more than 10 seconds, I think I almost lost a finger print), but the closer my crystal got to it the more pissed off it got. It would glow bright red, the heat emanating off of it increasing, and my hand would get heavier, my two crystal halves making this sort of gravely sound. Maybe this is their way of a pissing contest? What was really interesting though was what Mark told me happened when Andrew tried to touch it. According to Mark, when Andrew placed the tip of his blade on the crystal, that's when his powers fully awakened. Maybe it really hates people with the dark crystal? I wouldn't blame it.
