Title: Chapter 14
---
Mark's head throbbed, the pressure pushing up against his eyes. He slowly scanned his surroundings, his vision still distorted. In front of him, a lake calmly rested, its surface filled with autumn leaves from the surrounding trees. The sun emitted a red hue as it began to descend into the distance, its final glow before night began. To his right, a colorful curtain stood just a few feet away, being held up by two metal polls. He groaned, moving to stand up, only to realize he was currently chained to a tree. Mark could feel the dry blood on the back of his head, his hair all matted, and tried to ignite. Nothing happened. He peered down his shirt, only to see the crystal at its dimmest state yet, light barely pouring out of its many cracks. He tried to ignite again and again, but to no avail. He was stuck. A sharp stabbing sensation vibrated through his skull, the effects of his concussion worsening. He squeezed his eyes shut, hoping to eliminate the pain, but he had no such luck. When he opened his eyes, a familiar face stood before him, smiling down on him.  
"Good morning sunshine!" Andrew exclaimed, crouching down to meet Mark's eyes. He turned slightly and looked at the setting sun behind him. "You are quite the heavy sleeper, I'll tell you that. Had me worried I killed ya for a second!"  
"Fujmnk yohu." Mark slurred.  
Andrew struck Mark, backhanding him square in the jaw, a trickle of blood leaving his mouth.  
Andrew grabbed Mark's face and moved in close, whispering in his ear. "Do you think you're better than me? Do you think you deserve better than me?" He dropped Mark's face. "You're nothing compared to me." He stood up, and swiftly landed a kick directly to Mark's stomach. Blood shot from Mark's mouth, the air in his lungs following. The crystal still did not react.  
Andrew began to pace, his eyes moving frantically. "No I'm not going to kill him yet," he whispered, almost like he was talking to someone. He let out a big sigh, almost like a child would when told no. "Fine." he pouted, as he moved behind the tree. Mark felt the chains on him loosen, before fully coming off. He didn't hesitate, immediately shooting up and sprinting for the treeline to his left. Unfortunately for him, his brain did not agree with the plan, as he collapsed after only a few steps. Mark reached out his hands, and gripping onto the ground, began to pull himself forward.  
Andrew chuckled, watching how pathetic Mark looked at that moment. He placed his foot against Mark's back and pushed him down into the dirt, stopping him from crawling any further.  
"You can't leave before we play our game!" Andrew said, grabbing Mark's arm and flipping him onto his back. "How else will you win your prize?"  
Mark looked deep into Andrew's eyes for the first time, and he was terrified by what looked back. Not a single spark of humanity laid behind those eyes, the human shine completely eliminated. Mark went deeper, using what little power he had to feel the blood coursing through his veins. He found nothing. The boy in front of him was a mere corpse playing pretend. To Mark, he was no longer human. He needed to be erased. Mark did feel something, however. Not from this monster, but from behind the curtain. He felt blood seeping into the soil below, the stream getting slower and slower.  
"Here's the rules," Andrew was in Mark's face again, snapping his focus back. "Land one hit on me, just one, and you get your present."  
Mark slammed his already messed up head directly into Andrews, which, as one would assume, only intensified Mark's own pain. Andrew stumbled backwards, shocked by Mark's sudden attack. Using all of his strength, Mark got to his feet and began to stumble towards the curtain. Something felt horribly wrong, and Mark didn't have time to play any games. His double vision returned, and a wave of nausea washed over him, yet he persisted. Andrew just stood back, watching, his forehead cracked open with no blood seeping from it. Mark gripped one of the metal rods with his hand, stabilizing himself. With his free hand, he ripped the curtain open.  
"Isn't it a good surprise?" Andrew shouted from behind. Mark fell to his knees, vomit falling onto the bloodied earth in front of him. He couldn't look. It was a sight that he would never imagine that anyone would have to see. In front of him laid a metal pole, a now lifeless body affixed to its shaft. Large nails pierced her overlayed hands, holding up her body. Her entire figure was covered in cuts, as if she was someone's heavily used cutting board. Her mouth, disfigured into a disgusting grin. Directly above her navel, a dark crystal was trying to dig its way inside of her, like a bug burrowing into the dirt.  
"Molly." he sobbed.  
"She was broken, but I'm fixing her." Andrew now stood behind Mark, his hand on Mark's shoulder. "I think she'll be perfect."  
For the first time in a long time, Mark's mind cleared itself. It felt as though he was watching himself from afar, similar to how this story is currently being told. He wasn't in that moment, but merely a bystander. He felt the piercing eyes watching his back, just as he did in those dreams. Just then, Mark learned the eyes had a voice.  
"Ignite."  
Mark felt an arm reach around and touch the crystal, snapping it out of its stasis. Suddenly he was back, and without even realizing it, ignited the blood that coated his back. Andrew was flung backwards, his hand going up in flames.  
"There you are!" he screamed, scrambling to get up onto his feet. He was too slow. Mark grabbed his face and proceeded to slam Andrew's head into the ground repeatedly, half of his upper body covered entirely in fire. Mark said nothing, he only screamed and screamed, relying on nothing but instincts to keep him conscious.  
"My turn." Andrew spoke, his voice muffled by the roar of the flames. Mark felt a sharp object pierce the left side of his torso, followed by another in his leg and arm. Before he knew it, his whole left side was filled with knives, the sudden force of them pushing him away from Andrew. Andrew got to his feet, frantically patting the fire off of him. "My powers have grown considerably since we last fought," he tapped the crystal on his wrong, the veins surrounding it pulsating black. "A precious gift from my benefactor."  
The knives dislodged themselves from Mark's body, as they floated towards Andrew, circling around his head like a halo. Mark ignited the holes left behind, sealing his new wounds. Though he was stopping the bleeding, the constant ignitions and blood loss would catch up to him sooner rather than later. He had to end this now. Fighting against the constant stabbing in his head, Mark rushed forward. Knives began to fly by him, as he was somehow able to effortlessly dodge each of them. He ignited a small amount of blood on the ground next to Andrew, the concussive blast knocking him off balance. He was closing in on Andrew, only a few feet away and he would win. Mark knew he had to go for the kill, he couldn't allow himself to see the being standing in front of him as human. If he hesitated for even a second he would lose. Only 10 feet away, Mark reached out his hand, ignition in progress, ready to grab onto the monster and not let go until the job was done.  
Mark felt a cut. In an instant, both of his achilles were slashed, putting an end to his only shot of victory. He began to fall, only to be caught by Andrew, who held him up by his hair. Without hesitating, Andrew grabbed one of the knives still floating above him, and slit Mark's throat.  
"This is pretty pathetic." Andrew sighed, Mark gagged, unable to pull in any air. "I mean, you've been going around beating up all these 'bad guys', and for what? You clearly haven't grown any stronger. I told you he wasn't cut out for it!" He shouted at no one.  
Mark was trying to ignite, trying anything to stop his neck from bleeding, but nothing except a spark came out. He was all out of energy, all out of time.  
"Come on, that can't be it." Andrew whined. "What if I do this!"  
The pole Molly was hung on began to rise. Mark reached out his hand, as if to try and grab onto his friend who was too far away. He wanted to beg. He wanted to get down on his knees and plead with this monster to stop. He could still feel her life. It was faint, but maybe she could be saved. Even if it meant having that disgusting crystal burrowing its way inside her to take over. Anything to keep her alive.  
And then she was gone. The pole flung itself, with Molly still attached, into the depths of the lake. Mark stopped fighting. He had nothing left. No more fight to give. Even the crystal began to fade.  
"That did nothing, really? Andrew began to sound more angry then upset. "Are you serious?" his head was tilted, leaning in to hear the nonexistent voice. "This is bullshit, he's a failed test, have I not proved myself?" Andrew threw Mark onto the ground and sat on top of his stomach. "The boss wants me to do one final test, but if this doesn't work I'm letting you bleed out right here, you disgusting worm." Andrew took the knife, still soaked in Mark's blood and stabbed down into the crystal.  
A new wave of cracks spread throughout the crystal. A small, insignificant piece broke off, something that someone would have never noticed.  
The eyes watched in anticipation.  
The man in the shadows smiled.\
