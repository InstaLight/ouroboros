[[X]] doubled over, his whole body screaming out in pain. X had experienced pain in the past, but it was but a small pinch compared to this. It was as it someone was tearing away at a piece of his flesh, as agonizingly slow as possible. [[The Child]] rushed to X's side, grabbing onto his shoulder and asking "What's wrong, is everything alright?" "*Something horrific just happened.*" X responded, before vanishing without a trace.
X appeared on Earths moon, collapsing from the now persistent pain. Two bodies, no, corpses, laid before him. His nieces, [[Lux]] and [[Luna]], two beings that were once brimming with life, were now lifeless husks. He crawled his way to Lux, digging his fingers into the rough exterior of the moon, and place his hand on her chest. His eyes widened. X was not unfamiliar with death. Accidents would happen, failed experiments with planets would lead to unfortunate explosions, and every once and a while one of his children would temporarily lose their life. However, as long as an individuals soul is in tact, bringing them back was always possible. X scrambled over to Luna, who was just a few feet away, and examined her just as he had done with her sister. Tears began to form in his eyes. Both of their souls were gone. He focused his gaze towards Earth, just as another wave of indescribable agony washed over him.

The three siblings stared in horror as their youngest sister reached out to them, begging for help. Before any of them could react, [[Aqua]]'s form completely broke apart, and a wave of water hit the ground. "Reduced to a puddle," [[Grimm]] remarked, "how cute." Grimm's blade began to change shape as the remains of his pathetic excuse for a niece drenched its cold steel, morphing into a saber. "She had little potential to begin with, so there was no point in pushing her to her limit," Grimm began to taunt, "a drizzle may turn into a storm, but at the end of the day, it's just water." [[Zephyr]] rushed forth in a fit of rage, increasing the Earth's atmospheric pressure and slicing through Grimm with his wind. Grimm rushed forward, attempting to end the fight in a single swift strike, but ended up swinging at dead air. "Tricked ya," Zephyr snarked, and quickly released a barrage of slices on Grimm's back, sending him flying. In an instant, [[Terra]] created a stone wall in the way of Grimm's trajectory, causing him to slam into the newly formed mass. Before giving him time to recover, [[Solaris]] rushed over, releasing molten lava from her core, as Zephyr rapidly cooled the surrounded area with his gusts. Before Grimm could even realize what was happening, he was encased in a tomb of obsidian, with spikes protruding from the walls, directly into his figure. The pain was unlike anything Grimm had ever known. His smile grew. *Perfect*. He freed himself from the obsidian, breaking through like it was glass. Immediately upon escaping, Zephyr was on Grimm in a flash, once again sending a barrage of cuts directly into him. Every time Grimm would try to reach out or swing his sword in retaliation, Zephyr would simply vanish and reappear somewhere else nearby. His near invisible body made it impossible from Grimm to follow his movements, and even though Zephyrs attacks weren't the strongest, if he kept up this pace Grimm would surely collapse from exhaustion. Solaris shouted to her brothers "We have to fall back and wait for father to arrive! There's something wrong with Grimm, he'll know how to help!" "You're crazy if you think I'm going to just ignore this monster and run like a coward," Zephyr shot back, glaring at his sister. "I'm going to slice him up until there's nothing left to revive!" Grimm began repeatedly stomping the ground, causing a cloud of dust to form around him. Zephyr moved in for another volley of slashes. "*I just have to keep this up*" he thought to himself confidently, "*I can kill this psycho, without the help of dad or anyone else!*" Breaking through the cloud of dust, Zephyr was met with a terrifying, soul crushing smile looking directly at him. Due to Zephyrs constant release of wind, he unknowingly gave away his position as the dust was constantly pushed away from him. Zephyr gasped as the steel was driven directly through his soul, unable to think for one final time before collapsing. Once again, Grimm's sword changed shape, molding into a longsword. Hundreds of tons of stone began crushing Grimm, their mass increasing by the second. Grimm looked up to Terra rocky "face" twisted in an expression he had never seen before. Rage, sorrow, fear, all etched into his stone exterior. "*It seems my hypothesis was correct,*" Grimm thought gleefully, "*when faced with true danger, our evolution is sent into overdrive, causing us to grow stronger than we could ever imagine!*" Grimm once again broke the stone like it was nothing, and grabbed Terra by the neck, launching him into the air. "Stay away from my brother!" Solaris shouted, firing a blinding beam of flames directly at Grimm. Grimm dissolved into the shadow emitted from the blast, appearing directly behind Solaris. "Not yet. You still have potential." He whispered, before knocking the orb of light away as if she was nothing more than a pebble. Grimm shot up into the sky, catching Terra by the leg, and began to drag him to the dark space that lied above. "Why," Terra sobbed, "why would you do this to your own family? Your own kind?" Grimm's smile lessened for just a second, a wave of rage washing over him. "My own kind?" he spat back, "You all make me sick, you're not even strong enough to truly harm me, but you think that we're even slightly comparable?" Terra turned his leg into as many jagged gems as he could, piercing Grimm's hand. Instead of dropping the rock god, Grimm swung him upward, grabbed Terra's body with his other arm, and pulled him into a "hug". "Cherish this, you brute," Grimm flashed his wicked smile, "this will be the first and only act of 'love' you will receive from me." With that, Grimm's sword claimed another soul.

X could not believe his eyes. In the space between the moon and Earth, X watched as two of the most important beings in his life fought to kill one another. His first creation, shining brighter than X could have ever imagined, her flames so intense that space itself began to heat up, and his other half, his brother, slowly fading away, trying to block the blast with a large broadsword. Grimm, barely holding on, spotted his brother, and his grin twisted. "Please brother, you have to help me!" he begged, calling out to X, "she went crazy and started killing your kin, I can't stop her alone!". Solaris, unable to hear over the roar of her own flames, pushed on, inching closer to the god of death, her flames intensifying. Suddenly, she felt two hands grab her, pulling her into a warm embrace. "*Solaris, my beautiful daughter, please tell me what's happening*." X's words caused her to stop for just a moment, his calming voice breaking through her fury. He looked at her with concerned but loving eyes, and all of her emotions washed away, before she snapped herself back to reality. By the time she regained focus, it was already too late. X floated away in terror. Grimm, overflowing with joy, stood in front of X. His terrible sword, the one that had claimed so many lives, melted away, revealing its final form; a scythe appeared in death's hands, it's blade jagged and shaft as pitch black as the space surrounding them. "Brother!" Grimm exclaimed, "You made it just in time! That maggot of yours gave a truly brilliant final stand! Can't say the same for the rest of them." "What are you talking about," X stuttered, "What the hell did you do?" Grimm's smile no longer showed any malice, instead showing a frightening amount of excitement. "I figured it out my dear brother! All this time I thought there was something wrong with me, but I had the answer right inside of me all along! When you first described the sensation of love, I never understood it, but now I see clearly! I love the feeling of absorbing another's soul, because it reminds me of my time with you! The more of our mistakes I destroyed, the more whole I began to feel! Don't get me started on the rush of strength that once gets when merging with another soul too, it's absolutely intoxicating! Here's the kicker though; by pushing the worms to their limit's, I was able to evolve their strength beyond what we thought was possible! In return, they pushed me, leading myself to also grow in strength! Combine those two factors together, and I've amassed strength that could rival our creator! And now, with this wonderful weapon of mine, I can absorb your soul and we can be whole again! With our newfound strength, we'll be able to kill that horrible child so that he can never separate us again!" He was talking nonsense. None of it made sense. He felt love when taking the life of another? X was aware of his appointed title, the god of death, but it still made no sense. Death was supposed to welcome souls when their time had come, it wasn't supposed to seek to end life itself. X couldn't let himself get absorbed. He had to find a way to save the children, no matter the cost. Just then, both X and Grimm noticed a presence. The child was hiding the shadow's overhearing the conversation. Tears were streaming down it's face. Using the moment of distraction as an opportunity, just as Grimm had done a moment ago, X rushed in and grabbed onto the scythe, trying to cut a hole directly in Grimm's chest. Grimm's strength however far surpassed X at this point, and he was able to redirect the slash into his brother. Something curious happened in that moment. X's soul did not get absorbed by the blade. The attack did injure his body and soul, but the two remained connected together. Not wanting to waste this miraculous second chance he was give, X put everything he had into forcing the scythe's head into its owner, slicing his chest open. All at once, the souls of the once dead gods began rushing out and returning to their bodies. X howled out in pain. Dazed and facing true death, Grimm looked towards the child and began to beg. "Please child, you can heal us! I'm sorry for everything I said, I was drunk with power and knew not of what I meant. Please spare me this one time!" The child had heard enough. It sobbed, just as it did all those years ago when it broke it's first creation. It didn't know how to fix this. It didn't want to fix this. The child began to glow. Now, Grimm has a unique ability. He lacked proper eyesight, so instead he saw things through the amount of power they emitted. The stronger an individual was, the larger their outline was. Whenever Grimm would look at the child, his aura was immense, but nothing insane. But, in this moment, everything changed. The child's aura became the only thing Grimm could see. It filled every inch of space, and kept expanding. Grimm had severely underestimated the child's strength, and realized that even if he combined with X, there would be no point in even trying to fight the child. He needed to get stronger. He would never get the chance unfortunately. Just as quickly as it's aura expanded, it disappeared. The child, the one constant in the universe was gone. "Where did that bastard go-" his arm vanished. And then his leg. His whole body began to disappear. "What the hell is happening here?" He looked around frantically. He could see the other's aura's, but they too were beginning to fade away. He turned his attention towards X, who was looking back with a sense of pity. "Don't worry brother, one day we will be whole again. I promise you that." His form completely vanished, leaving behind nothing but a small core, the outer shell of his soul. The others soon followed, all being reduced down to their most basic self. X was the only one that remained, though he too was slowly fading away. Acting with haste and purpose, X began collecting the cores, including his fallen brother, and began planting them on Earth. "*One day*," he thought, "*one day, I'll be able to look up at the stars again with my children. One day I will be able to experience love again. One day-*" he closed his eyes, and world faded away.
