Title: Chapter 3
tags:

---
The knife burrowed into Mark, pulling itself clear through his body. It exited cleanly out of his back, causing blood to spray onto the nearby foliage. Mark looked down at the gaping hole in his stomach, his blood gushing out, life slowly fading. "*Is this guy serious?*" he thought. Somehow, his attacker from earlier in the week was not only back, but was now armed with a magical knife that *gained* momentum as it hit its target. How was that fair? "*He's wide open now, if I can just knock him out I can get out of here.*" Mark rushed towards Andrew, his adrenaline keeping him from passing out. He felt it again. The knife had somehow found its target once again, the force of the blade propelling him towards Andrew. It felt as though an external force was pulling on it, sending Mark flying towards its owner. Andrew held out his hand and caught Mark by the neck, which in turn removed Mark's ability to breath. Mark looked down and was horrified. The boy holding him was barely recognizable. His hair was filled with dirt and twigs. His skin was covered head to toe in goosebumps, and he was sweating like it was 100°F out, despite fall being just around the corner. His eyes were wild like an animal, and he couldn't even make eye contact with his prey. He was mumbling to himself, and Mark strained to listen. "Gotta find it, where is it? He said he would have it, it has to be here." Andrew kept whispering over and over. Mark couldn't hold out any longer, his vision going dark as he watched Andrew begin to tear his shirt off.

Something was off. The last time he almost died (though this shouldn't become a regular occurrence), Mark felt sorry for himself. He was pathetic. This time was different. He didn't think of his loved ones. He didn't think of how uninteresting or unimportant he was. This time, he only felt one emotion. Rage. Mark's eyes snapped open. He was on the ground now, and watched as Andrew slammed his knife down, aiming directly for Mark's chest. Mark put his left hand up reflexively, the blade sliding through his palm like it was made of butter, with the handle of the blade getting caught on his bones. Mark looked up at Andrew, and then down at his chest. The crystal. He was aiming for the crystal. Mark pushed back, trying to stop the blade from touching the gem. He couldn't overpower his attacker. He was still too weak. The tip of the blade made contact.

First, a warm sensation spread throughout Mark's body, the kind of feeling you get when you take a hot shower after coming inside from a snow storm. It burned, but it felt great. Then came the boiling. It was like every blood cell in Mark's body was suddenly hurled directly at the sun, and then siphoned right back into him. The pain lasted for just a second, but to Mark it felt like eons. He begged to be killed, to be put out of his misery. These feelings however were replaced almost immediately. The feeling of rage was back. It began to consume him. Finally, ignition occurred. Flames burst from where the knife had pierced Mark's palm, covering his entire hand. 

The ignition caused a small concussive blast, sending Andrew flying back, dislodging the blade from Mark's hand. Mark seized the opportunity and jumped to his feet. He stared, in both wonder and horror, as his body worked rapidly to repair itself. The cut on his hand had practically disappeared, the flames licking away at his wound. The piercing in his stomach was slowly healing itself as well, flames erupting forth from there as well. It seemed that everywhere Mark's blood had touched was now covered in a magnificent blaze, the poor forest breaking out in a wildfire for a second time this week. The crystal burned a crimson red, its glow pulsating with Mark's own heartbeat. Despite the terrified look in Andrew's eyes, his smile persisted, and without hesitation he rushed back towards his opponent. Mark was prepared this time, and was able to dodge the incoming blade, weaving out of the way. Andrew was wide open, and Mark took his first swing, the punch landing directly on Andrew's arm. The boy howled in pain as the flames engulfed his arm, and swung his blade once more in a desperate get back. The blade grazed Mark's cheek, causing more flames to come forth, the pain only increasing their ferocity. Just as Mark went in for another strike, the flames on his fist died, causing a flat blow to Andrew's chest. Seizing the opportunity, Andrew shoved Mark back with tremendous force, putting distance between the two. Mark wasn't even trying to think anymore, he wasn't trying to understand what was happening. His body was moving on pure instinct, as the rage guided him to the right path. Andrew broke their silence, shouting "I need that stone Mark. He said he would help me if I could bring it to him." He sounded desperate, almost like he was about to cry. If Mark didn't know any better, he would almost feel sorry. He didn't know what Andrew was talking about, but if his way of asking for help involved numerous stabbing attempts, then maybe he should figure things out on his own. Mark's flames had almost completely disappeared at this point, the wound on his stomach almost closed and the flames on his cheek on their final breath. Despite the flames intensity, they had no real range to them, meaning that if he wanted to win this duel, he had to get up close again. Andrew on the other hand still had his knife trick, so with the current distance between the pair, he had the upper hand. The tree bark crackled as the flames began to spread from tree to tree. The boy's eyes were locked on one another, both unmoving, like two cowboys in a standoff. In an instant, the blade burst forth from Andrew's hand, soaring through the gap between the two. Mark was prepared this time. He dodged to the right, leaving his left arm behind, exposing it to a direct attack. He allowed the blade to slide along the length of his arm, the cool steel creating a thin cut from the inside of his elbow to his palm, allowing his blood to flow free. His left arm now positioned behind him, Mark ignited the blood on his palm, using the concussive blast to send himself forward. Reaching out with his right hand, Mark grabbed ahold of Andrew's neck, slamming him to the ground. Andrew's smile slipped for the first time, Mark finally having the upper hand. The boy's hair fell back, exposing a tiny shard of *something* lodged into his forehead, the dark fragment emitting an ominous feeling. Mark allowed the blood from his recent cut to flow freely onto Andrew's face. One ignition, and the boy would surely perish. "*This isn't me*," Mark thought to himself, watching Andrew scream in terror as the blood crawled along his face. "*Am I really going to kill him?*" he questioned, unsure if he was even in control of his body right now. The rage was intoxicating, his body screaming at him to finish the job. Mark outstretched his left palm, which was still actively burning, and positioned it centimeters from his opponents face. He was going to do it. "*IGNIT-*" The earth rumbled beneath the boys, interrupting Mark's activation, Mark heard the whistle of the blade returning to its owner, allowing him to roll off of Andrew and out of the way before getting hit. Suddenly, the ground split open, a wall of stone breaking up for fight. "That's enough," a voice shouted from behind, "you don't want to become a murderer today." Mark turned to look, only to see *him*. It was the guy from school who had been watching him every day at lunch, his eye's constantly locked on Mark. The boy walked towards Mark, continuing speaking "let's work this out like big boys now, use our words instead of our fists." Mark raised his palm ready to fight his next opponent. Mark however, forgot to take into account that he had lost almost a liter of blood from his multiple active wounds, and despite the fact that those wounds were now healed, the blood was still very much missing. With that, the adrenaline wore off, and Mark succumbed to his blood loss.
