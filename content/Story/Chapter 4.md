The orb floated in the distance, emitting a soft glow that cut through the black void. Despite its distance from him, Mark could still feel its brilliant heat, beating down on him. Behind Mark, a series of bizarre dreams played out, the type of dreams you get when you come down with a nasty fever. Mark broke free from those dreams however, and was now being drawn closer to the orb. Every step he took felt like he was wading through a sea of syrup. His legs were heavy, and his movements were sluggish. The heat intensified with every step, and he felt his skin begin to boil. He continued to trudge through, but it just felt like the orb was getting farther and farther, while at the same time it continued to get hotter and hotter. His body was telling him to return to the dreams, to go with their flow until he would eventually wake up, but his mind persisted. Mark stopped dead in his tracks. The heat was sweltering, but he suddenly went cold. Someone, *something*, was watching him. It's eyes peering from an unknown location. He looked up to his left. He saw-

---

Mark jolted up, gasping for air. His body was drenched, like he just had a bucket of water thrown over him. Standing above him was the mysterious boy from school... holding a bucket. He smiled sheepishly, "You were really burning up there, I thought you were about to burst into flames! Sorry about that."

Mark blinked confused on what was going on, where he was, how he got here. Up close, the boy was nowhere near as menacing as Mark thought he was. His usually intense look was now replaced with a ridiculous grin, and his whole demeanor was completely flipped. If you saw a 6'4" high schooler with the build of a professional offensive lineman, who watched you every day, staring at you like you killed his dog, you probably would've had a negative first impression too.

"I should introduce myself, the name's Vincent, and I'm really sorry about the water!"

He took Mark's hand and started to shake it violently. His strength almost crushed all of Mark's bones, and Mark fought to stifle a yelp. Mark felt something else however, something besides this kid's unnatural strength. A familiar sharp stabbing pain, coming from Vincent's palm. Mark's expression must've given away what he was thinking, and Vincent quickly pulled away, letting out a sigh.

"Let's get this out of the way now before we go any further," he said, flipping over both of his hands, exposing them for Mark to see. Mark's eyes widened. In the center of Vincent's palms lay two crystals, their rough exterior protruding outwards ever so slightly. The crystals appeared to be two halves, and unlike Mark's crystal they did not emit any sort of glow, but it looked as though something was moving around inside of them.

Mark got out of the soaked bed and began walking around the room, reaching for a towel that was hanging on the door. The bedroom was covered in old movie posters, the floor littered with dirty clothes. He grabbed the towel and began drying himself off,  his eyes scanning the room, as Vincent's gaze following him as he wandered around.

"Are you looking for a way to cut yourself?"

Vincent asked in such a sweet tone that it caught Mark completely off guard.

"I've figured out how your powers work, so I've taken the liberty of removing any potentially dangerous object from the room." Vincent grinned, looking proud of himself.

Mark lunged for the doorknob and swung the door open, only to be greeted with a wall of solid concrete. Mark whipped around, shouting, "Who the hell are you and what do you want with me? You've been stalking me for the whole week and now I'm trapped in your room with you? What's your problem?"

Vincent, surprisingly, looked visibly upset from that last remark. *This guys got issues,* Mark thought.

"There's nothing wrong with me," he retorted, "I just wanted to have a calm conversation without freaking you out."

"And you thought locking me in a room with you would keep me from freaking out?"

Vincent's face got bright red as he looked at the ground. This guy was a total wimp!

"We got off on the wrong foot," Vincent recomposed himself, "you're free to leave, but I'd really preferred if we could talk."

Vincent walked past Mark and gently laid his right hand on the wall. The concrete began to reform, and it appeared as though it was sinking into his house. Soon enough, it was like it wasn't even there at all. Mark sighed and sat on a nearby chair, his shorts still soaked. "Where's Andrew? Where am I? What do you know about the crystals?"

Vincent beamed, and began getting out a clear pair of clothes for Mark.

  

---

  

Mark had quickly changed into a pair of clothes that were clearly too big for him and sat back down. Vincent cleared his throat and began explaining.

"Andrew disappeared quickly after I separated you two. You had passed out from blood loss, and when I rushed over to secure him he had just vanished. No trail of blood, no foot prints, nothing."

Mark buried his head in his hands. Vincent, noticing Mark's movements, quickly added, "In my experience, you should be safe. For now, at least."

Mark looked at Vincent with a puzzled expression. "What do you mean, 'in your experience'?"

The boy looked frazzled for a moment, but quickly composed himself. "I'll get to that. After I noticed he was gone, I focused on setting up a small perimeter around the fire to keep it from spreading. That thankfully had worked, and there was only one tree that had burned down. After that, I grabbed you and carried you to my car, and now we're here!"

"Dude, you kidnapped me."

Vincent just stared. "That's besides the point," he waved his hands around, "I used the emergency call feature on your phone to let your mom home that I was a friend from school and I was watching out for you. She was upset but seemed fine with it."

Mark's poor mother, the amount of stress that she must've gone through this week worrying about her darling son. No mom should have to deal with this!

"Anyway's, as for where you are, you're at my house, as you probably guessed by now. If you're wondering the location, we're about 30 minutes from school, right outside of North Philadelphia!"

Mark leaped from his chair and rushed to the window, peering through the glass. *There's no way,* he thought to himself. He was a brisk 5 minute walk from his house.

"Hey, you live around here too, huh?" Vincent chirped up, "we're basically neighbors!"

Mark sat back down, and Vincent launched into his final speech, "So, for our last order of business, the matter of these annoying ass rocks in my hands. I got them around a year ago-"

Mark interrupted, "You've had them for a year?"

Vincent, sighing obnoxiously, responded "It's rude to interrupt people you know. But yes, I have had them for about a year. It was last summer, and I was walking alone on my favorite trail, about 20 minutes from here. Halfway through, my walk gets ruined by this terrible thunderstorm! I start booking it back, because at this point I'm close to an hour away from my car, when suddenly I hear a kid screaming for help! I rushed over to where the sound was coming from, and sure enough there was a little kid, all by herself, about to get swept up in a mud slide! So, as anyone would do, I pushed her to safety, but I ended up getting swept up in the mess! I thought that was the end of me, I couldn't see, I couldn't breathe, but eventually I came to, surrounded by these giant stone pillars, keeping the mud from drowning me, and these freaky stones were embedded in my hands!"

Mark was encapsulated by his new ally's story, the heroic tale moving him! It was certainly more heroic than what happened with himself.

"It didn't take long before I figured out what these bad boys could do. I can manipulate the geological structure of any mineral that I touch. There's some limits to it though. For example, it wears out my body big time! Nothing as gruesome as what you have to deal with, but I feel like my bones are going to shatter whenever I use it, especially for bigger structures! Also, I can't create anything greater than what I would be able to physically lift. For instance, if I could only bench 400 pounds, then that's the limit to the amount of mass that I can work with. Thankfully, it seems like the crystals also super-charged my muscle growth, so I've been increasing the limit every day!" Mark would be lying if he said he wasn't jealous, but he kept any comments he had to himself. Vincent's face dropped, and he started to glare at Mark.

"I had about a week before they started to appear. Everywhere I went, it felt like someone was always watching over my shoulder, and not in the good guardian angel kind of way. I went back to the trail, to try and clear my head y'know, but the feeling only got worse. Before I knew it, some psycho started lunging at me, speaking nonsense and trying to claw into my hands. I tried to resolve it peacefully but he just wouldn't give up." Vincent looked to the side, a tear forming. "I hit him back, and I guess I didn't know how to control my new strength. He stopped breathing after one punch. I ran off to get help, but by the time I came back he was just gone." His lip was quivering and his voice started to break. "It just kept happening. It felt like every day, every time I was alone, some new freak with a funky ability was coming after me and the crystals. I never wanted to hurt any of them." He was full on sobbing at this point. "I couldn't bear it anymore. I just wanted it to stop, especially before it got to the point of my family getting involved. I tried to end it myself, but I couldn't even do that right. Anytime I tried, my skin became hard as stone."

Mark was never the best with dealing with emotional people, he usually just shut down, but something was different this time. He instinctually reached out and brought the boy into a hug. Mark's warmth spread to him, and his sobs softened. They broke apart, and Vincent smiled at Mark.

"Each day I lived in fear, fear that I might get attacked, fear that I might take another life. But one day it just stopped. For about three months I lived with no attacks. The pain was still there, but at least I no longer had to worry. But then, he showed up. The man in the suit. At first I thought it was just a coincidence, just some suit that looked like all the rest. I kept seeing him though. On my way to school, when trying to go on a walk, when taking my sisters to the park. He would always be there, watching with that stupid smile on his face. Like he was taunting me. I tried to go after him one time, but it felt like the universe didn't want me to reach him. Some lady hit me with her bike, I tripped and fell on some uneven concrete, a bus stopped right in front of me. By the time I got to him he was gone without a trace."

  

---

  

The boys talked for a while longer, as Mark changed the subject and started asking more personal questions to get to know Vincent better. He learned that Vincent has two sisters, his favorite manga is "Dragon Ball", and his favorite actor is George Lopez. By the time the sun was setting, Mark was walking down the street back to his house, promising to meet up with his new friend over the weekend. Mark arrived home and greeted Nara, before seeing his mom sitting on the couch waiting for him. She said nothing, and patted the couch cushion next to her, signaling to Mark to come and sit.

"I was worried sick today, especially after I heard the news about that horrible boy that hurt you broke out, but I was glad to hear that you had a friend watching your back. He sounded like a very nice young man. Now, what was it that you wanted to talk about?"

Mark had completely forgotten. With everything that had happened, it slipped his mind that he promised to tell his mom about the crystal. He ran his fingers over it, feeling its warmth through his shirt. He thought about what Vincent had said, about not getting his family involved. He inhaled deep, and looked at his mom. "I think Molly hates me."
