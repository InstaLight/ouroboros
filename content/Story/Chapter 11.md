"I'm gonna burn her face off."  
It had just crossed the hour mark of our lovable protagonists being stuck in a stone shell of their own making, and Mark was already losing his mind.  
"So violent," Vincent replied. "I am feeling quite cozy if I do say so myself." He laid with his hands behind his head and feet perched against the wall and let out a deep yawn. "I think I'll sleep until she tires herself out, thank you very much."  
Before this started, Mark had been having a pretty good day. He got a 72 on an exam he thought he bombed, his mom had packed him an extra Oreo cookie for lunch, and today he would be racing in a special invitation only meet that Coach had put him in. Which started in 30 minutes. And was over an hour away from his current location.  
"I can finish this in five seconds!" Mark shouted, trying his hardest not to throttle his partner. The pair were currently up against a woman who could throw up bullets., and could absorb and regurgitate any incoming projectiles. This had, believe it or not, not been the strangest ability they had gone up against this week. Upon arriving on scene, the two were immediately bombarded by a barrage of bullets, with their only solution being confining themselves to a small protective rock shell surrounding them. Vincent had tried to restrain her in the same way he had with the porcupine boy from just a few weeks ago, however there simply was not enough material to work with in the cramped office they were currently in.  
"This all stops once that home-wrecking whore shows her face!" the lady screeched, a quick release of ammunition following shortly after.\  
"I'm telling you dude we just gotta play the waiting game," Vincent dramatically stretched his arms, making an unnecessary moaning sound.  
"Wait a minute, is that thing getting closer?" Mark heard the lady's muffled voice from just outside the shell.  
Mark's phone buzzed for what seemed like the hundredth time in the past hour of waiting. He didn't need to check to know that at least a dozen of those notifications were from coach, with frantic texts and calls from Tim making up the rest.  
"Let me out of this right now or I swear to god I'm going to-" Mark stopped, feeling a hard wall pushing against his back. Vincent let a sly smirk make its way onto his face. Vincent placed his hand on the left of the stone shell, which transformed in an instant. What had been their home was now an unbreakable prison for the woman, the stone encasing her entire body, leaving only her head free to move.  
"I'll fucking kill you, you little shits," the lady spat at them, "you think you're so tough because you have some cheap costumes on? You're both pathetic. When I got out of here I'll-"  
The stone slowly moved upwards, covering her mouth. "Girl you're crazy, no wonder your husband cheated on you." Vincent snarked before turning to Mark. "I know, I'm the best."  
"I don't even know what you just did." Mark responded exhausted.  
"Well it was actually pretty clever you see-"  
"Dude, you know I love you, but I really gotta go, you gotta save that for later." Mark began to jog backwards, getting ready to make his escape.  
Vincent pouted under his mask. "Fine whatever I didn't even want to tell you anyways."

Tim couldn't tell who was more nervous, him or Coach. It had been 40 minutes since the John McGucket 5k invitational meet was supposed to start, and Mark was still nowhere to be seen. According to Coach, it had been 20 years since their school last won the invitational, and if Mark, the greatest athlete he has ever trained (his words not Tims) didn't show up, he had no other option but to kill himself. He'd been stalling for as long as he could, which apparently included stealing and hiding multiple kids' spikes, but their time was just about up. THe racers were all on the starting line, and the starter was walking into position.  
"This race is looking a little empty, don't you think?"  
Tim, practically about to start crying from stress, looked up to see Mark casually standing by his side. He opened his mouth to speak, to yell, to say anything, but Mark just slapped him on the shoulder and jogged into place on the starting line.  
"Wish me good luck, okay?" Mark yelled back. Any stress Tim had in that moment had faded, and all Tim could think about was how thrilling the coming race was about to be. To his left, Coach was on his knees thanking the heavens.  
Despite the fact he had practically sprinted 15 miles to get to the meet on time, Mark was barely exhausted. He felt guilty about that, the fact that some of these kids were prodigies, and trained tirelessly for this race, yet he would still come out on top because of something he stumbled on by accident. It was moments like these that made Mark wish he could just turn off the crystal's power; he didn't care if he won or not, he just wanted to finish using his own strength for once. At the very least, he would hold himself back so the lead wasn't too crazy. Lost in thought, Mark failed to realize that the gun had fired over two seconds ago, and now had a poor coach begging him to start running. With a sheepish smile, he started out, breaking ahead of the pack. Just like all races, Mark let his mind wander. How was the crystal making his natural strength that much greater? Was his body working like an engine, with small ignitions inside of him causing his enhanced speed and strength? It was a silly thought, but who could know for sure. The number of power enabled humans was increasing every day, and the media couldn't get enough of it. It seemed like every news station had a different name for this new category of persons, but the one that Mark liked the most was "oddities", which he and Vincent had heard on a podcast while driving to school. He preferred titles with neutral connotations, due to the fact that apparently most of those "infected" with these strange abilities actually weren't bad people, despite what he'd been encountering themselves. In most other areas in the world, people who wake up with their newfound powers have been admitting themselves to hospitals, or isolating themselves from others. The pure number of abilities was staggering as well, it seemed like there were no two people with the power. He heard about a guy in Russia that could turn inanimate objects into sentient ice creatures if he ate enough snow.  
"Hey, there's a nice breeze up here!"  
Snapped out of thought once again, Mark shot a look to his right, his eyes finding a boy jogging next to him, like he didn't have a care in the world. Before Mark could say anything, the boy reached out his hand.  
"The names Shun, and I've heard a lot about you recently!"  
Mark fully turned his head to make eye contact with the boy, quickly examining him. He was about the same height as Mark, perhaps slightly taller, with thick straight black hair, which was currently slicked back with sweat. The way he was running was so weightless and free, it was like he was drifting through the air without a care in the world.  
"Shun?" Mark thought, the name striking a chord with him. He felt as though he heard it somewhere before, but the memory was just out of his reach.  
"It's kind of crazy," Shun continued speaking, pulling away his hand before Mark could shake it. "Usually someone of my skill would have some history behind them, but you just kind of poofed in out of nowhere? I heard you finished your first race in a little over fifteen minutes! That's practically unheard of. Makes you wonder," he paused and glared towards Mark. "If there's more to it than skill." Mark's heartbeat quickened, his throat dry. Suddenly, it felt as though a large weight was placed on his back as he felt the exhaustion of a race for the first time. Despite his cockiness, Mark felt no ill intention from the boy. Well, at least not the same kind of intention he felt from those who were always trying to kill him.  
"Performance enhancing drugs are a crazy thing, but there's no way in hell I'm letting that determine this race." Shun declared, a feeling of pride behind his voice.  
"Drugs?????" Mark almost laughed out loud. This kid thought he was on some kind of steroid.  
"So what, I can't just be naturally gifted?" Mark defended. This clearly struck his opponent, as Shun clenched his teeth and focused ahead. Mark grinned. Forget about what he was thinking earlier, feeling bad about beating others with his powers, he had to take this cocky guy down a notch, and if they were evenly matched physically, then he had to win the mental battle instead.  
"Sorry to break the news to ya, but you might just not be as good as you thought you were. Maybe-" Mark tripped. In all the excitement, he had failed to remember that he was in the middle of a race on uneven ground. His foot had caught on something, and he was now plummeting towards a gravel downhill, gaining momentum as he tumbled. He hit the bottom with a crunch, his new "rival" not even giving him a passing glance as he ran past. Getting scratched up in gravel was the worst. The little rocks get in all the crevices of your wounds and really mess you up. Mark would have to get Vincent to use his powers or something later to get them out. He now laid motionless at the foot of the hill, as more people passed him by, trying to recover the air that was knocked out of him. Slowly, he got to his feet, scratches covering his arms and legs, as he tried his hardest not to activate his ability, as he let the pain from a simple injury like a scrape sit with him for the first time in a while. Mark sighed, the race already over for him. Even if he waited for all the other runners to pass, leaving him alone to activate his ability without anyone seeing, it would be too late to catch up to that asshole Shun. Mark grumbled under his breath.  
"Coach is already pissed at me for being late, so I guess he can't get any more upset at me."  
He began to shuffle through the course, the final runner finally passing him. Just as he was about to activate his flames, he heard the unfortunate sound retching in a nearby bush. Peaking around the corner, he saw a scrawny kid in a singlet on his hands and knees, with a small puddle of yellow bile in front of him.  
"You alright over there?" Mark asked, approaching cautiously.  
"Dont stop for me," the kid gasped, before beginning to gag again. "I'm a lost cause, just keep going."  
"I'm a lost cause myself," Mark replied, "I'll wait for you and we can walk to the finish together, how does that sound?" he reached down his hand to help the boy up.  
"Thanks man, I really appreciate it," the boy replied, wiping the remaining vomit from his mouth. He took Mark's hand and got up on his feet, finally looking at Mark. "Woah, you're pretty beat up yourself." His eyes began to drift downward, focusing on Mark's chest. The following actions occurred simultaneously, as if the two individuals were playing out parts they had rehearsed dozens of times. The boy lunged forward, his mouth opening wide to reveal hundreds of rotating teeth. Mark leaned backwards, igniting the wounds that covered his body, sending himself and the boy flying away from one another. Mark's back slid across the gravel floor, causing further scraping. The boy slammed into a nearby tree with a thud, and crumpled up on the ground.  
"Can I please just go one day without this." Mark asked as he stomped towards the boy, his left arm still ablaze.  
"STAY AWAY!" the boy screamed, curling himself into a ball. Mark stopped, confused. "I know you," he whispered, almost too quiet for Mark to hear. "Not your face, but that feeling. The burning. He told me about that thing inside you."  
Mark looked down at the crystal embedded in his chest, its red glow flickering ever so slightly.  
"I thought it was all a bad dream, but now I've seen it firsthand. I didn't mean to attack you, my body just moved on its own. I can feel him in my head, trying to make me do things I don't want to."  
Mark couldn't stand seeing someone in this much pain, especially when there was nothing he could do about it.  
"Okay, I'll leave, but I just have one question." Mark inched slightly closer, getting down on one knee. "Do you know his name?"  
The boy shook as if the question itself had hurt him. "Nightmare." he spat. "He's a goddamn Nightmare."\

"Yeah man I don't really think that's a name." Vincent mumbled, his mouth filled with Cool Ranch Doritos. Mark was retelling the races events as they had what Vincent called their "weekly grounding session", aka Vincent eats Doritos in the grass while Mark stresses about their next moves. "Wait nevermind, this website shows there's [five](https://www.mynamestats.com/First-Names/N/NI/NIGHTMARE/index.html)." He shoved his phone in Mark's face, dust still covering his fingers.  
"This plan was stupid anyways," Mark flopped on the ground, exhausted. "How were we supposed to find him based on his name anyways? How do we even know he is a real person."  
Vincent stuck out his bottom lip, his eyes closed like he was deep in thought. "Yeah I guess you're right, it was worth a shot though." Rolling onto his stomach, Vincent began licking his fingers. "Do you wanna know what I'm thinking?"  
"Probably something stupid." Mark responded, closing his eyes as well.  
"Im thinking we just gotta wait for him to come to us."  
Mark thought about saying something snarky back, but he knew Vincent was right. How do you find a guy that probably isn't even human?  
"I bet if we keep beating up bad guys," Vincent continued, "he'll have to show his face! He'll probably be like 'ugh, my little minions can't get them? Guess it's my turn!', and then BAM, he'll show up, we jump him, and then everyone infected goes back to normal, including us!" He didn't sound as confident about that last part. Mark thought it was a reasonable plan, but hated the idea of essentially just waiting around. Sure, they could get all those violent bad guys off the streets, but there was nothing stopping new oddities from being created. The thought of his mother, or Tim, or.... The thought of anyone else he knew getting infected by Nightmare made him sick. Mark stood up, brushed off his shorts, and looked at Vincent. "Well then, guess we gotta take down as many baddies as soon as possible huh? And I guess we should make a big show out of it to get this Nightmare guy's attention? So I've been thinking," he paused for dramatic effect, "about our hero names."  
Vincent paused mid lick, his eyes wide. "Say you're being for real right now."
