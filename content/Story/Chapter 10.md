Mark walked through the halls, his eyes blank, the sounds around him muffled. There was no reason for him to be as upset as he was about the whole Molly situation, but he couldn't help it. It felt like he was being stabbed over and over by an annoying invisible elf. It also didn't help that out of the past five "missions" he and Vincent have been on, four of them have resulted in getting their asses handed to them. The only time that they actually did something was when they found a twelve year old trying to rob a candy shop. He didn't even have any powers like the owner reported, he was just a normal kid who started bawling his eyes out the moment the duo showed up. The media hadn't decided on a name for them yet, and they've instead been relegated to being the center of jokes on radio talk shows. The police were currently trying to ID them, but Vincent insisted they had nothing to worry about (this of course, did not ease Mark in the slightest).  
"Are you even listening to me right now?"  
Mark snapped his eyes into focus, Tim walking beside him with his usual concerned look. "Come on dude you can't still be upset about this," he said, "it's been a week, if she's not thinking about it you shouldn't be thinking about it."  
"And how would you know what she's thinking?" Mark shot back.  
Tim put his hands up, "I don't, but you still shouldn't be sulking around like this, sorrows a bad look on you."  
Despite Mark's "star" status, his popularity had died down pretty quick. Some kids were still unusually friendly to him, but for the most part everything was back to normal.  
"I'm fine, okay?" Mark insisted. "Just got a lot of other stuff on my mind is all."  
Tim looked doubtful, but laid off. "How about this," he proposed, "let's go see that new Venom movie that's coming out this weekend, that'll get your mind off things!"  
Mark rolled his eyes. "Dude I heard its ass."  
Tim opened his mouth to start an argument, but paused and looked down the hallway. Confused, Mark followed his eyes, seeing a crowd beginning to form. The pair marched down the hall, pushing past people in the way. At the center lay a guy, a sophomore by the looks of it, curled up on the floor with his head between his knees. About four feet away from him lay another kid, knocked out, blood running from his nose.  
"I see him every night, just watching me." the sophomore moaned, rocking back and forth on the floor. "And every night he comes closer and closer and closer and closer and-" he picked up his head and looked at the unconscious kid. "I thought it was him. Oh my god I thought he found me here, I thought it was him. What did I do, what did I do oh my god."  
No one standing around dared to get close to him, to check if the kid knocked out was alright. They all just stared at him, some people even recording. It was disgusting. Mark began to push through, when he felt some try to pull him back by his arm. Tim's eyes were wide, a silent begging on his face.  
"Someone has to step in." Mark whispered, before throwing Tim off him. Mark finally went through the inside barrier of the circle, people backing up as he approached his fellow classmate. He knelt down, putting a hand on the guy's shoulder.  
"Hey," he spoke softly, quiet enough for just the two of them, "tell me what's going on."  
The boy continued to shake like a leaf, his nose dripping with snot from sobbing. "I can't stop seeing him everywhere. Every time I close my eyes he's there. When I walk around my neighborhood, I feel like he's hiding in every shadow. Like he's mocking me. He wants me to be reduced to this."  
"Who are you seeing?" Mark replied, a lump forming in his throat.  
"He's always in that fucking suit." The sophomore looked at Mark for the first time, and the two locked eyes. He sniffled, wiping away the tears from his eyes. "I'm so sorry, I didn't mean to-". His eyes trailed off, looking over Mark's shoulder. His face morphed, a look of pure horror washing over him. He started kicking the floor, backing himself up against the wall of lockers behind him. His mouth hung open, his face distorted, as if he was a baby right before throwing a tantrum. He shot up his right arm and pointed directly over Mark's shoulder, a gut wrenching scream escaping his throat.  
"WHAT THE FUCK, WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT, WHAT THE FUCK, STAY AWAY PLEASE JUST STAY AWAY." He began scratching at his face, blood beginning to spill from beneath his finger nails. Mark fell backwards, rapidly looking over his shoulder and back. There was nothing behind him. Nothing that he could see at least. Finally, a group of teachers arrived, two holding the sophomore down while another checked up the unconscious boy.  
"All of you get to class now!" One of them yelled. Tim rushed in and grabbed Mark.  
"Come on man, we gotta go," he said. Mark had no response, and the noise around him went silent once again.

---

Fall was Mark's favorite season. All of the seasons have a distinct smell, and to Mark, falls were the most pleasant. He watched the leaves as they began to fall, their distinct orange coloring signaling the start of October. Tim kept looking back at Mark, trying to make sure he was okay, but Mark never met his gaze. He began running his finger over the crystal, feeling its rough edges through his shirt. What could he have seen behind me? Mark wondered to himself. Was it the man in the suit? Or could it have been something else entirely? Mark's phone began to blow up, leaving him with no option other than taking a peek at it. Vincent was sending ten texts a second, all saying the same thing: "Meet me in the ceramics room now." Annoyed, Mark responded "I'm in the middle of class, I can't just leave." to which Vincent responded with "If you aren't here in five minutes I'm emailing the entire school a picture of your Sailor Moon collection."

---

"You couldn't have waited 30 minutes?" Mark asked, storming into the ceramics room. Vincent sat at a nearby pottery wheel, his hands covered in clay as he carefully molded a small vase.  
"Why must you come into my happy place so angry," Vincent replied calmly, taking his foot off the petal and walking over to the sink to rinse his hands. "Can a man not see his best friend?"  
Mark looked around the room, a nearby shelf filled with unbaked pots and plates. "Did you make all those?" Mark asked.  
Vincent perked up, "Yeah, I took ceramics last year and my teacher really liked my shit so she just lets me come up here whenever I want now. Anyways, don't change the subject, we have to talk about what happened with you and Joey."  
"Who the fuck is Joey?"  
Vincent, who seemed prepared for that response, already had his phone pulled up with Snapchat opened. He began scrolling through story after story, all showing the same thing from different perspectives: Mark, trying to talk to "Joey", before he began to freak out even more. "Did your breath really smell that bad?"  
"That's not funny." Mark replied, deadpanned.  
"It's a little funny. Anywho, this little incident is all anyone's talking about now, and it's leading to some pretty disturbing revelations."  
Mark, puzzled, asked "What do you mean?"  
"What I mean is that I've heard at least five other people today mention a certain man visiting them in their dreams every night. Could this just be a knee-jerk response? Maybe. But the fact that he's being mentioned more and more is pretty chilling don't you think?"  
"It's definitely related to all these super powered people showing up out of nowhere, but what could it mean? Why would he be in random people's dreams?" his mind began to wander, before suddenly snapping back with a disturbing revelation. "Daisy. That fucked up girl that I fought in the alley. She was saying something about having nightmares for weeks before she got her powers and met you."  
Vincent let out a long exhale, processing this new information. "Okay so if that psycho is anything to go off of, it looks like we got some serious potential issues on our hands. If seeing this freak in your nightmares turns you into a crazy super human, then we got at least half a dozen in the making just at this school." He began pacing back and forth, his hand on his chin.  
"Genuinely what do we even do in this situation?" Mark asked. "We got multiple ticking time bombs on our hands, who knows how many people are seeing this guy. We still don't even know who or what he is, we don't know why any of this is happening in the first place, and I feel like we're completely powerless to do anything against it." He didn't know what to feel. He wanted to yell, angry at whatever messed up fate put him in this situation. He wanted to curl up into a ball and cry, just hide away from everything. At the same time, he wanted to do something. Anything to help these people that are suffering, or people that may suffer in the future. He thought of his mom going through this kind of torture and it made him sick. "We have to do something, Vinny. We gotta get this guy."  
Vincent pulled Mark in for a hug, his still wet hands soaking Marks back. "We're in this together dude, no matter what. We may not be related by blood, but these crystals mean something. We're brothers."  
Mark had never had a brother. He never had any siblings, or any kind of friendship that was this important to him. They had known each other for a month, yet it felt like they've been a part of each other's lives from the beginning. Maybe it was just the crystals and whatever freaky connection they had that was causing Mark to think this way, but he believed the feeling was real.  
"Yeah," Mark held out his fist. "Brothers."

---

The moment Mark stepped foot out of Vincent's car, Nara sprinted up to meet him, his mom sat on the front step waiting. She smiled, waving at Vincent. "Dude, you didn't tell me you had a hot mom." he said, speeding off before Mark could get a word in. His mom hugged him from the back, pulling him in close. "Wanna tell me about what happened at school today?"  
Mark turned around, shocked. "You heard about that?"  
"Oh sweetie, it's all over the school mom's Facebook group." her bottom lip began to quiver, as she pulled him back in for another hug. "You were so sweet to that poor boy. When everyone else stood around you took action to help a kid in need." She pulled away, stroking his hair. "You remind me more and more of your dad every day. He would be so proud if he saw what happened today."  
"You think so?"  
"Oh honey I know so. From the moment you were born, you were his whole world. I just wish you had more time with him. I have something I want to show you." She led Mark inside, Nara following them, and handed him a book that laid on the coffee table.  
"I made this a couple of weeks ago and was waiting for a good time to give it to you."  
The moment Mark opened the book, his eyes began to water. Every page was filled with pictures of a young Mark with his father. Pictures of him holding Mark in the hospital, their first trip to Disney World, passed out asleep on the couch together.  
His mom began wiping her tears. Mark rushed in, embracing her for the third time. "Thank you so much mom. I love you."  
She squeezed him. "To the moon and back."

---

Mark laid in bed, holding the book over his face, going through its pages for the fifth time this night. His favorite was one of the two of them at his dads fire house, a two year old Mark wearing a helmet way too big, his dad in full uniform. His father dedicated his whole life to saving people, people he didn't and never would know personally. People who may never give him thanks, people that may not even want to be saved. He was a hero. He died a hero. Saving others was the least Mark could do to maintain his legacy. "I'll make you proud." he whispered to no one but himself. "I promise. I'll save them. All of them."
