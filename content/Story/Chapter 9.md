Title: Chapter 9
tags:

---
Mariah hated Mondays. Who doesn't? The weekend ends and you have to get back to your mundane life, going through the motions until Friday rolls around. This Monday however, was particularly bad. For starters, she was currently experiencing the worst hangover of her life. The previous night, her boyfriend of four years, Tyler, admitted to her that he's been seeing someone else for the past four months. So, after "calmly" telling him to leave, she did what any emotionally stable adult would do, and immediately drove to her nearest Wine and Spirits to get the cheapest bottle of red wine she could find. Was drinking until she passed out on a Sunday night the smartest move? Probably not, considering it was now 10 AM and she was two hours late to her shift. Not to mention the horrible nightmares she got. She quickly threw on the first pair of clean clothes she could find and rushed out the door, only to be greeted with her least favorite sound in the world.  
"STOP!"  
Standing down the hall was her slimy, no-good, scumbag of a landlord Geraldine. She hobbled over to Mariah, the floorboards screaming with each step.  
"I heard some yelling last night, is there perhaps trouble in paradise?" She said, her discolored teeth on full display.  
"Is there something you need Geraldine, I'm really late to my shift."  
She cackled like some evil witch out of a Disney movie. "Is there something I need? Well since you asked so kindly, I would love it if you could give me your rent today. I get the fucking world is ending and everything, but mama needs a new tree for her cats."  
Mariah rolled her eyes. "You're being dramatic, the world's not ending anytime soon unfortunately. Now look I really have to-"  
"Tonight," her hot breath wafted over Mariah. "If I don't have the money by tonight, your ass is on the street."

---

Mariah's shift, believe it or not, did not make her day better. You would think with everything going on lately, like the world shattering news that super powers are real and that everyone that has them is an asshole, would disrupt normal life. Well, you'd be wrong. After being berated by her shitty manager for ten minutes and given her third "final" warning, she was immediately thrust into a hoard of angry patrons. For eight hours she ran from table to table, her head feeling like it was about to explode the entire time. By the time she finally got off work, she was racing the clock to make it to the bank. She was meters away from the door, when suddenly she tripped on herself, and she began hurling towards the ground. Right before impact, her arm was grabbed, and she was pulled into an embrace. Looking down at her was a decent looking man, with slicked back blonde hair, and a few scars around his face. She would be lying if she said she didn't get butterflies. Behind him stood a much taller man, who looked so stressed that he was about to break down crying.  
"You gotta watch where you're goin there miss," the man said. "Don't want you getting hurt now do we."  
Mariah tensed, her body going stiff. The man was holding something in his right hand, and while she couldn't see it, she could guess what it was. The nervous man behind him continued to nervously look around, as his body suddenly started secreting some kind of liquid.  
"If you just play along, you'll be home safe and sound in an hour."  
The three entered the bank, Mariah and the man approaching the teller as the nervous guy locked the door behind them. His body was completely slick now, light reflecting off of it like he was doused in oil. Everyone inside looked at the pair, a sense of recognition showing on their faces. The man chuckled.  
"Alright folks it looks like we all know what's going on here. I'm sure you've seen me and my associates' faces on the news these past couple of days. I commend all of you for still coming to work, knowing that we'd probably make our way here eventually. I'm sure one of you has already set off the silent alarm as well, so lets make this speedy okay? I want a clean $1,000,000 in cash, and then me and my friend are out of your hair! If someone tries to be a hero, the pretty lady dies. If one of you put a dye pack or any type of tracker in the money, the pretty lady dies. Simple?"  
Mariah's heart was beating out of her chest. Today, out of all days, is when fate decided to play with her? She felt the cold metal of the barrel pressed against her back, and she began to pray. She was never the religious type, but she thought that if there was a god, she might as well ask for help. The banks manager came from behind the counter, holding a large duffle bag. He marched over to the man with the gun, his eyes pointed at the ground, as he handed it over. The man flashed a smile. "Thank you sir, you have been the most corporative bank that we've visited in the past week. Y'know-,"  
The windows shattered, glass flying everywhere. The manager dropped the duffle, and Mariah screamed, tears beginning to flow freely. The nervous man backed up, shouting "We gotta go, now!"  
Through the window, two people emerged. On the left stood someone wearing a dark red jacket with the hood up, glass embedded in his knuckles, blood dripping down his fist. On the right, a stockier gentleman, wearing pants and a tank top, his muscles exposed. Both individuals wore face coverings, the left wearing a full face helmet, and the right an intricate oni mask. The man with the gun couldn't help but laugh.  
"What the fuck am I looking at right now? We got Daft Punk and his handler over here."  
The boy on the left's fist erupted into flames, their crackle serving as a response to the rude comment. His companion knelt to the ground, the floor's tiles cracking as they worked their way up his arm, acting as a sort of rock gauntlet. The man let Mariah go, a familiar smile appearing.

---

"I look so fucking stupid. I should've never said yes to this, this was such a dumb idea."  
Mark was currently standing face-to-face (face-to-helmet?) with a literal bank robber. The super hero stuff didn't get more generic than this. For the past four days, Mark and Vincent spent their free time on two things: coming up with "cool" superhero costumes, and trying to figure out where these two assholes would strike next. It took them two tries, but they were finally able to show up while the robbery was in progress this time. Mark's heart raced as he tried to recall the plan he and Vincent had set up for dealing with the criminals the previous day. Vincent raced because he was going to be on TV.  
Mark rushed the scared looking one, who already looked like he was pissing himself, while Vincent began to stop the one with the gun. Mark slapped his ignited fist into his right hand, using the broken glass still embedded to create more cuts. With both fists lit up like a bonfire, Mark went to punch his opponent and-  
He was laying face first on the ground, confused as to how the hell he got down there. His fist slid right off the man, the liquid surrounding him like an impenetrable barrier. He flipped on his back to see a horrifying sight; not only was the man completely fine, but the liquid he was secreting was apparently flammable, causing his whole body to be covered in Mark's flames.  
"A LITTLE HELP OVER HERE." Mark shouted at Vincent, as he rolled out of the way of his recently ignited foe's foot trying to stomp down on him.  
"I'M BUSY GIVE ME A MINUTE" Vincent shouted back. "EVERYONE GET OUT OF HERE NOW, I'LL HOLD HIM BACK."  
The employees (and Mariah) all sprinted outside, sirens quickly approaching from a distance. A wall broke through the ground, blocking off all the doors and windows.  
"It's just us now gentlemen," Vincent said, currently engaged in an intense standoff with his opponent, as Mark continued to roll around trying to get back on his feet. "How about you two act like fine upstanding citizens and turn yourselves in. I don't think you want to get into it with us."  
The man chuckled as he looked over at his partner and Mark, who were currently chasing each other around in circles, unable to harm one another. "Is that right?" he said in a teasing voice. "You're just a couple of kids playin' dress up after school."  
Vincent flushed under his mask. "I'll have you know that I'm no kid, I'm a man." His voice cracked a little.  
"Alright then big man," the criminal said, holding his arms out wide. "Hit me with your best shot."  
"Really?" Vincent responded surprised, dropping his fighting stance. Mark continued to punch (and miss) his opponent.  
"I swear on the Lord above that if you knock me out, I'll turn myself in. I can personally assure you that my partner will do the same."  
Everyone in the room paused. Mark and Vincent looked at each other. The scared guy, still actively burning, looked like he was about to throw up.  
Vincent shook his right arm, his rock gauntlet clicking against itself. "Don't say I didn't warn you," he said, dealing with what he believed to be the nastiest right hook of his life. Everyone stayed quiet. Beneath their masks, Mark and Vincent's mouths were agape. It was like Vincent's fist had hit a brick wall, as his gauntlet began to crumble. The man smiled, spitting to the side. "I think it's only fair if I get a shot now, wouldn't you agree?"  
"Oh shi-"  
Vincent went flying, taking a direct hit to his stomach, flying through the wall he had made just moments ago, exposing the bank to the outside world. Mark, stunned by what he just saw, got kicked in the balls. "This isn’t happening," he thought, laying on the bank's floor. The entire street was lined with cops, guns drawn, all now pointing at a knocked down on his back Vincent. He quickly put his hands up, signaling that he was friendly. "PLEASE DON'T SHOOT PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE"  
The two robbers stepped through the Vincent shaped hole in the wall, the scared man acting as a shield for his partner. The cops began firing, the bullets sliding off the man like they'd seen in that first news report. The two began sprinting away, duffle bag in hand, as a group of cops followed. Mark stepped through the hole to see Vincent, on his knees, with cops approaching to put him in cuffs. "HEY, OVER HERE!" Mark yelled, shocking the cops into looking in his direction. Seizing the opportunity, Vincent put up a new wall, separating him and Mark from the officers. The two began to book it, sprinting off down an ally, Vincent blocking off the path behind them as they went.  
"We gotta figure out a better way to get out of battles, this is just embarrassing." Mark said, sprinting ahead.  
"You know what's really embarrassing?" Vincent shouted back at him, "The news got that whole thing! Our first public appearance and we're already going to be a joke! Our reputation is down the gutter!"

---

"Ok, how about this," Vincent started, marker in hand as he began to write on a whiteboard. "The Boulder and Firestorm!"  
Mark rolled his eyes, packing up his things. "Those are your worst ones yet. Why are we even coming up with hero names in the first place?  
Vincent tapped the marker against his chin. "You know you have a point, the best hero names are the ones that the general public come up with! Hey where are you going?"  
"I gotta meet someone about a thing," Mark responded, his face going red.  
"You sly dog, you're hanging out with Molly aren't you?"  
"She's been wanting to talk for a couple of days now and I said today worked, she's expecting me."  
"Fine fine I'll let you off the hook, but we have to do an actual debrief tomorrow, ok? Especially if the news coverage tonight is bad, we gotta come up with ways to recover."  
The two fist bumped. "Deal." Mark said before leaving.  
Mark walked down the street, somehow more nervous then he was just an hour before. Apparently, his brain didn't know the difference between talking to a girl and being held at gunpoint. "We've been friends literally all of our lives, why am I even nervous," he thought. "This is just two friends, having a friendly conversation, doing friend stuff!" He rounded the corner to Molly's house to see her already sitting outside, waiting for him on the front step. The sunset was at a perfect angle, and from where Mark was standing, she appeared to be blessed by the heavens.  
"Fuck."  
Molly spotted Mark, her face lighting up as she ran down to greet him. She pulled him in for a hug, his heart trying its hardest to keep up. She pulled back, her eyes glistening. "I want to start off by saying I'm sorry with how I've been treating you these past few weeks. I've been nothing but awful." Mark's heart melted.  
"Ah, well, don't worry about it, really, high school is rough so I understand."  
Molly breathed a sigh of relief. "Oh thank goodness, I'm glad you understand. I just really wanted to make a good impression on our first day, and you know how you were, I just didn't want to get anything ruined y'know? I mean, I didn't want anyone spreading rumors about us or anything."  
Mark, a little lost already, replied "Yeah I guess that's fair."  
"To be honest, I've really missed spending time with you," she continued. "I was so caught up in trying to be popular that I lost sight of my one true best friend! It was really selfish and silly of me. You know, if you're fine with it, we could start driving in together again." She grabbed his hand, maintaining eye contact. Mark felt like he was about to explode.  
"Yeah that works with me," he mumbled, trying to get the words out.  
"I mean look at you now! Big cross country star, who would've guessed. You know everyone's talking about how amazing you are; you're really rising the ranks in terms of popularity! I think it'll be good if we're seen together!"  
Mark pulled away, taken aback. "Hold on, are you only saying this because you want to be popular too? Are you serious right now?"  
Molly, flustered, responded, "No of course not, don't be ridiculous, I'm just saying that there's something in it for both of us now that you know, you're cool now and everything."  
Mark could feel the tears forming in his eyes. "Are you fucking serious right now? Is your social status all you ever fucking think about? How shallow are you? Acting like we're best friends now that I'm 'popular' is pathetic. Seriously Molly, grow up." He turned around and began to storm off, trying his hardest to remain composed. He didn't even care if he made her cry. She was no longer a friend in his mind.

---

He watched the argument unfold, crouched, hidden by the shadow of a house. He watched Mark storm off, tears in his eyes. He chuckled. "Pussy," he said under his breath. The girl, Molly he thinks her name is, is crying too, as she sits on her front step, her head buried in her arms. He stands up, and begins to take a step towards her house.  
"Not yet," a voice says, the boy feeling a hand on his shoulder, keeping him from moving forwards.  
"She's wide open," he responded. "What would be a better time than now?"  
He turned around to look at the man in the suit, who was smiling down on him. "Give it a few more weeks," the man said. "He'll be strong enough by then. And when he is, he's all yours, Andrew."

---

Mariah laid on her parents couch, cars speeding by right outside the window. According to Geraldine, being used as a hostage is not an excuse for paying rent. Mariah laid on the couch, wondering about the two mystery men that came in and saved her. "They sounded like kids," she thought to herself. "Hell, they probably were. The world's all kinds of messed up right now." Eventually, sleep overcame her. Her mind began to wander, forming random shapes and images for her. Those images became dreams. Those dreams became nightmares. The Nightmares smiled at her.
