The Ouroboros mobile reader is now in TestFlight for iOS! This app is designed to be an easy and convenient way to read Ouroboros on the go, as well as providing push notifications when new chapters are out! Clicking the link below will bring you to test flight (or the App Store if TestFlight is not installed.) From there, you can install the Ouroboros Reader and start testing!

Small note: Due to the nature of push notifications being local at the moment, please keep the app open in the background so that it can check for new chapters and announcements. Thank you!
