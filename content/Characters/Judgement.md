**Judgement** is the god of Justice, and is the first living creation of [[The Child]] ([[1 - Creation]]). It lived alone with The Creator, before getting split into [[Grimm]] and [[X]].

# Appearance
Judgement has three defining features:
1. Dark, indigo colored eyes
2. A toothy smile that seemingly covers its whole face
3. Four arms
Unlike The Creator, Judgement can not change it's form.

# Personality
Judgement is a calm and level headed individual, opposite to The Creator. However, when it comes to other life forms, Judgement becomes extremely jealous, as it believes to be the perfect being, and it should not have to co-exist with others.
