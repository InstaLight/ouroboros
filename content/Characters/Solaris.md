Title: Solaris
---
Solaris is the goddess of flames, a child of [[X]], and  the eldest of her three siblings ([[Terra]], [[Zephyr]], [[Aqua]]).

# Appearance
Solaris frequently changes form, but is most comfortable simply as a ball of light. Her color changes based on the temperature of her flames, which are heavily connected to her emotions. 
# Personality
Solaris is generally a very levelheaded person, despite what the flames may lead you to believe. She cares deeply for her family, especially her siblings, and would do anything to protect them. She is naturally suspicious of others, leading her to appear to others as standoffish, but once you get to know her she's the sweetest person (god?) you'll meet
# Abilities
- *Flame Manipulation* - Solaris naturally emits extreme amounts of heat off of her body, which she can ignite at any time. Her flames can take many different forms, and she can control them as though they are an extension of her body.
