Luna is the goddess of shadows, a child of [[Grimm]], and the twin sister of [[Lux]]

# Appearance
Luna takes a humanoid figure, resembling her father. Her body is pitch black, with no distinct features. She naturally blends in with space, allowing her to be a perfect spy!
# Personality
Luna's personality is difficult to describe due to the fact that she curates it to whoever she's interacting with. She is a master manipulator who can talk her way out of any situation, except when it comes to her father. You'll know when you've truly become friends with her, as her relationships are ride or die, and she will be by your side until the very end.
# Abilities
- *Shadow Manipulation* - Darkness is Lunas power. Similar to Lux with her light, Luna can absorb darkness and use it either as a straight up offensive attack or to forge items. The more darkness she absorbs at once however, the more corrupt she becomes, leading her to suffer from memory loss, among other side effects
- *Shadow Leap* - can dissolve into and move through shadows at extreme speeds, he uses it as a form of teleportation
