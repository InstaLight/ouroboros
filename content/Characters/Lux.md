Lux is the goddess of light, a child of [[Grimm]], and the twin sister of [[Luna]].

# Appearance
Lux takes a humanoid figure, resembling her father, sporting a pair of large feathered wings. Her body constantly emits a subtle glow, though she can change its intensity with little effort.
# Personality
Lux is a very timid and defensive individual. The sound of a branch cracking could shock her so bad that she would pass out on the spot. Though she is timid, she still has a heart of gold, and will do everything she can to protect her sister, and anyone else who calls out for help in times of need.
# Abilities
- *Light Manipulation* - Don't let Lux's personality trick you, her light manipulation is not something that you want to mess with. By absorbing any source of light, natural or not, Lux is able to refactor the collected light into energy that can be used to heal, used as a direct attack or forge a variety of items. Her most common is her signature Shield of Light, a shield with defense that scales to the amount of light that she has collected. If she has a constant stream of light, good luck breaking her down!
