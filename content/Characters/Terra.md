Title: Terra
---
Solaris is the god of minerals, a child of [[X]], and  the second oldest of his three siblings ([[Solaris]], [[Zephyr]], [[Aqua]]).

# Appearance
Terra body is made up of a variety of minerals, leading him to being a multicolored lump of rocks. Due to the constantly creation of minerals in his body, he is always producing new types, however he must also occasionally shed these minerals to prevent himself from growing too large.
# Personality
Terra is the perfect example of a gentle giant. He can grow to the size of an adult planet, and yet care for even the tiniest of creatures. Terra is constantly wound up, always stressed about something, but this stress also allows him to constantly be on his A game. If you need something done to perfection, he's your guy.
# Abilities
- *Mineral Manipulation* - While Terra does naturally produce a random assortment automatically minerals, he can also concentrate and make specific minerals that he needs. Also, any minerals that were ever produced by him can be manipulated by him in any way he pleases, as long as he is in direct contact. He can change one type of mineral to another type (as long as they are of equal value), and he can clump minerals together to form rocks, allowing him to create structures.
