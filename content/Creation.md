# Story
In the beginning, there was a child. Floating in a void, the child had no thoughts and no dreams, it only knew the vast emptiness that surrounded it. The child spent its centuries simply sleeping, never aging, never experiencing anything. 
One day, as the child awoke from its rest, for the first time in its life, it spotted something. Drifting only a few feet away was a small speck. The speck was nothing of interest, it held no special properties or importance, nothing more then some dust. Despite how insignificant this dust was, the child was enamored. It floated around the dust, meticulously examining it from every possible angle. Eventually, the child stretched out its tiny arm and gave the dust a light tap. Upon the point of contact, the dust vanished, leaving no trace that it had ever existed in the first place. The child wept. It cried for days, days that turned into weeks, weeks to months, months to years, and years to decades. All the child knew was the dust, and it wished nothing than for it to return. Eventually, sleep took the child once more. For the first time, the child had a dream.
Upon opening its eyes, the child was met by a most wonderful surprise. Everywhere, for as far as it could see, dust filled in its once empty space. Filled with excitement, the child once again reached out for the dust, but just as before, the clump vanished. However, unlike last time, the child did not cry. Instead, it was filled with determination. The child reached out for another clump, this time pouring all of it's energy into holding onto the dust. When the child opened it's palm, a single speck remained. The child smiled, overwhelmed with joy. As it hugged the dust as tight as it could, a light began to emit from it. This light, this beacon in the child's eternal darkness, would end up being the catalyst that would change everything.
In the following decades, the child would become fully aware of the power it possessed. Every time it dreamed, more dust would appear. Every time the dust would appear, it would make the clumps illuminate. The more the child performed this action, the more it's curiosity grew. The child began experimenting with the strength and hue of the light that the dust was emitting, eventually leading to the discovery of colors. The more the child created, the more it dreamed, and the more powerful it grew. The child, however, yearned for more, more than just this colorful dust. Using the dust as a base, the child began shaping new life. 
Time passed. Million of failed experiments were created. None of the specks ever gained life. The child's dreams never faded though, it would find a way to create life. In a final attempt, the child reached out and collected all the dust it dreamed up over its life, and poured its whole heart into it. Left weak and tired, the child fell back into it's slumber.
When the child opened it's eyes, it saw its beautiful creation looking right back at it. It starred at him with beautiful iris colored eyes, and a grin that spread across its whole face. With tears of joy in its eyes, the child hugged his creation.
As the child and its creation grew together, they began to develop ways of communicating telepathically. They quickly learned that the creation did not have the same ability to create new matter that the creator possessed, so the creation took up a new role. It would act as the creator advisor, helping it come up with new things to create. Because of this role, the creator gave its creation the name of [[Judgement]]. 
Judgement and the child would continue to create, building themselves a place to live, stars in the sky, and various other smaller inventions. While they lived a happy life together, the child yearned to create more life. Whenever it would try and gather the dust as it did before, only a lifeless husk would be formed. Judgement saw the child's frustration, and recommended that it should stop with its obsession. Unable to bare seeing its creator in distress, Judgement revealed that it was the reason the child was unable to create new life, as it would interrupt the process whenever the creator slept. Judgement grew jealous and believed that it was the only life form that the creator needed. In a fit of childish rage, the creator grabbed its creation and ripped it in two. After realizing the horrific act that it performed, the child desperately scrambled to fix its beautiful creation. It cried out, pleading for Judgement to return. It vowed to never harm one of its creations ever again. Just as the child was about to give up, the two halves of Judgement began to glow, taking on new forms. In its right hand, Grimm, the God of Death, was born, with X, the God of Balance, being born in its left hand. The two new gods each adopted a feature from their previous form, with X taking Judgement's large, curious eyes, and Grimm taking its permanent smile.
As a gift, and apology, to it's two new gods, the creator bestowed the ability to self-create offspring. Grimm created two daughters, a goddess of light named [[Lux]], and another of darkness named [[Luna]]. X created four children, being [[Solaris]], [[Terra]], [[Aqua]], and [[Zephyr]]. The child began working with it's new family to create our solar system, filling every corner of the once empty void with wondrous creations and life. Finally, the child felt truly satisfied.
