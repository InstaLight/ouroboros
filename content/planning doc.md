**Ouroboros book 1 arcs**
- Heroes rising

- Introduce mark, Vincent, then nightmare as the main villain of the arc, then Molly
- battle between andrew and mark as the final fight of the arc
- ARC SHOULD END BEFORE OCTOBER 27TH 

- Sandman arc

- Introduce sean (zephyr), siobhan (lux)
- Sean is introduced as a vigilante looking for the “sandman”, someone with the power to kill nightmare
- X is reintroduced at the end of the arc, discovered by lux 
- X shows mark the future, causing the first break

- End of dreams arc

- Builds up to a major fight with nightmare
- Tournament style, each character gets their own one of one fight
- X saves mark by stopping time temporarily, causing the second break
- Nightmare escapes on the brink of death and the squad follows
- Finds that nightmare has been creating powerful souls for the purpose of feeding Grimm to bring him back to power
- Get introduced to berserk mark
- Arc ends with Grimms revival

- Return of the king

- Grimm is back but relatively weak
- Team tracks him and keeps an eye on him
- Grimm, after corrupting some FBI agents that have been investigating the appearance of powered individuals, finds marks identity
- After returning home from a fight, mark sees his home burning to the ground
- He rushes inside to save his dog and mom, Grimm sneak attacks, mark tried to block but gets his left arm cut off
- A battle ensues, rest of the squad pulls up
- Grimm slaughters all of them
- X/Mark goes berserk, kills Grimm but nearly kills himself too
- X offers mark the opportunity to rewind time, which he takes
- Time rewinds by 10 minutes, locking in the loop and causing the third and final break
- X/mark is able to force Grimm out of his host body, but mark gets shot by a blast of concentrated darkness
- Nightmare, who was purified by lux by receiving part of her soul, reverts to luna and grabs mark before the pair dissapears

END OF PART 1

  

- Rebuilding

- Mark is inside of Lunas pocket dimension to regain strength
- Learns how to imbue items with elemental energy
- Escapes to find that a year has passed 
- Reunited with everyone

- FBI

- Mark is arrested an accused of the murder of his family
- FBI knows who mark is and tries to interrogate him about the identities of the others
- Escapes custody but spends rest of series on the run

- The Last Dance

- Grimm, who has been “inactive” since marks disappearance, re-emerges
- Covers the entire city in a eternal darkness, summoning the undead to steal souls for him to grow stronger
- Another tournament style battle ensues
- Mark and Grimm fight
- Grimm, in his dying breathes realizes his ability to open rifts between universes
- Pulls his other universes souls into himself, creating the Reaper
- Reaper kills Molly
- Mark/X rip the souls out, reverting the Reaper to Grimm
- Mark goes berserk one final time, but it’s not enough, and he dies before Grimm is finished
- Taking full control of his body, X summons his children and Lux/Luna all into marks body, creating a bomb
- X takes Grimm up into space, destroying them and all of the elementals

- END OF BOOK 1
